# Fasteners, Joints, and Bearings

## Fasteners

### Takeaway
Fasteners are standardized, removable or permanent hardware that clamp or locate parts; selection depends on access, load (tension/shear), environment, and reusability, with dimensions/materials governed by ISO/ANSI/ASME/ASTM standards [ANSI Fastener Standards](https://webstore.ansi.org/industry/fasteners); [Machinery's Handbook via Wikipedia, Bolt (fastener)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolt_(fastener)).

### Cited Findings
- Bolts are externally threaded fasteners intended to pass through an unthreaded hole and be fastened with a nut; screws mate with a preformed or self-formed internal thread — [Wikipedia, Bolt (fastener)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolt_(fastener)).
- A bolted joint can carry load by clamping friction (preload) and/or bolt shank bearing in shear; needs holes/access and controlled preload — [KEVOS joining-method comparison](https://kevos.com/insights/choosing-a-joining-method/).
- Rivets are permanent fasteners: a shank+head deformed into a second head; strongest in shear, can take tension — [Wikipedia, Rivet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rivet).
- Pins/keys/retaining rings locate and retain without threads; rings take axial load, pins take location/shear — [Ideambox retaining rings & pins](https://ideambox.com/assets/downloads/retaining-rings-pins.pdf).
- Over 90% of fasteners are steel — [Wikipedia, Bolt (fastener)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolt_(fastener)).

### Inferences
- Threaded fasteners dominate where serviceability is needed; rivets/pins/keys/rings dominate where permanent or precision location is needed.

### Gaps
- No single source gives all load ratings; ratings are size/material/standard-specific and must be taken from manufacturer/standard tables (flagged per entry).

### Hex bolt / hex cap screw
- Description: Externally threaded fastener with hexagonal head, driven by wrench/socket; may have unthreaded grip length under head for shear doweling — [Wikipedia, Bolt (fastener)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolt_(fastener)); standards ISO 4014/4017, DIN 931/933, ASME B18.2.1 [Fastener Technical Manual](https://classccomponents.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Fastener-Technical-Manual.pdf).
- Load capacity: Tension via proof load (property classes e.g. 8.8/10.9 metric, SAE grades 2/5/8); shear via shank bearing. High axial clamping with preload — [Wikipedia, Bolt (fastener)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolt_(fastener)).
- Advantages: High strength, reusable, wide size range, easy torque control.
- Limitations: Requires nut and access to both sides; can loosen under vibration without locking; hole needed.
- Applications: Structural steel, machinery, automotive, equipment assembly.
- Select when: Removable joint, moderate-to-high loads, controlled preload needed.

### Socket head cap screw (Allen)
- Description: Cylindrical head with internal hex socket; fully threaded or with grip length; high-strength alloy steel — [Fastener Technical Manual (DIN 912/ISO 4762)](https://classccomponents.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Fastener-Technical-Manual.pdf); [Fastener standards cross-ref](https://eicac.co.uk/fastener-standards/).
- Load capacity: High tensile/shear; small head allows high preload in confined spaces.
- Advantages: Compact, high strength, clean appearance, recessed drive.
- Limitations: Socket can strip; needs hex key; less torque than hex head for large sizes.
- Applications: Precision machinery, tooling, fixtures, aerospace (varies).
- Select when: Limited wrench clearance, high strength, compact assembly.

### Flat (countersunk) head screw
- Description: Conical head seats flush in countersunk hole; Phillips/slotted/hex drive; used where protrusion is unacceptable — [Fastener Technical Manual (DIN 965/ISO 7046)](https://classccomponents.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Fastener-Technical-Manual.pdf); [UF fasteners lab notes](https://web.mae.ufl.edu/designlab/lab%20assignments/eml2322l-fasteners.pdf).
- Load capacity: Moderate; head bearing distributes load in thin sheet; not for heavy tension.
- Advantages: Flush surface, good appearance, aerodynamic.
- Limitations: Weaker head, harder to drive, countersink required.
- Applications: Sheet metal, aircraft skins, consumer products.
- Select when: Flush surface required, light-to-moderate loads.

### Round head machine screw
- Description: Domed head with slotted/Phillips drive; mates with tapped hole or nut; common in small assemblies — [Fastener Technical Manual](https://classccomponents.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Fastener-Technical-Manual.pdf).
- Load capacity: Low-to-moderate tension/shear.
- Advantages: Inexpensive, simple, no special tools.
- Limitations: Low torque capacity, head protrudes.
- Applications: Electronics, panels, light machinery.
- Select when: Light loads, general assembly, low cost.

### Carriage bolt
- Description: Smooth rounded head with square neck under head that bites into material to prevent rotation; used with nut — [Wikipedia, Bolt (fastener)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolt_(fastener)); [Fastener Technical Manual (DIN 603)](https://classccomponents.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Fastener-Technical-Manual.pdf).
- Load capacity: Moderate tension; shear via shank; good in wood/soft materials.
- Advantages: Tamper-resistant (no external drive), self-locking against rotation, smooth head.
- Limitations: Requires square hole/soft material; lower strength than hex bolts.
- Applications: Wood structures, wagons, fencing, docks.
- Select when: Wood/soft materials, smooth exposed head, one-side tooling.

### Stud
- Description: Headless externally threaded rod; one end screwed into part, other takes nut — [UF fasteners lab notes](https://web.mae.ufl.edu/designlab/lab%20assignments/eml2322l-fasteners.pdf); [Fastener Technical Manual](https://classccomponents.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Fastener-Technical-Manual.pdf).
- Load capacity: High tension; avoids repeated thread wear in parent part.
- Advantages: Repeated assembly without damaging base threads; precise preload.
- Limitations: Requires tapped hole; removal can be difficult.
- Applications: Engine blocks, flanges, pressure vessels.
- Select when: Frequent disassembly, threaded holes in soft/cast parts.

### Hex nut
- Description: Six-sided internally threaded nut mating with bolt/stud; standard grades match bolt property class — [Fastener Technical Manual (DIN 934/ISO 4032)](https://classccomponents.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Fastener-Technical-Manual.pdf).
- Load capacity: Matches bolt tensile strength; thread stripping is design limit.
- Advantages: Simple, high strength, reusable.
- Limitations: Can loosen under vibration; needs locking if dynamic.
- Applications: General bolted joints.
- Select when: Standard removable clamping.

### Nylon insert lock nut (nyloc)
- Description: Hex nut with nylon insert that deforms over threads to resist loosening — [Fastener Technical Manual (DIN 985/ISO 7040)](https://classccomponents.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Fastener-Technical-Manual.pdf).
- Load capacity: Same as hex nut; prevailing torque locking.
- Advantages: Vibration resistance, reusable limited times, no separate washer.
- Limitations: Temperature limit of nylon; prevailing torque; not for very high temp.
- Applications: Automotive, machinery, vibration environments.
- Select when: Vibration/loosening risk, moderate temperature.

### Flange nut
- Description: Hex nut with integral washer flange (sometimes serrated) to distribute load and resist loosening — [Fastener Technical Manual (DIN 6923/ISO 4161)](https://classccomponents.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Fastener-Technical-Manual.pdf).
- Load capacity: Moderate; larger bearing area than hex nut.
- Advantages: No separate washer, faster assembly, some locking via serrations.
- Limitations: Flange can embed in soft materials; not for extreme loads.
- Applications: Automotive, sheet metal, general assembly.
- Select when: Need load distribution without separate washer.

### T-nut
- Description: Nut with flange and prongs/spikes that embed in wood/composite to provide threaded anchor in thin panels — [UF fasteners lab notes](https://web.mae.ufl.edu/designlab/lab%20assignments/eml2322l-fasteners.pdf) (unverified specific design; general knowledge).
- Load capacity: Moderate pull-out in wood; depends on panel thickness.
- Advantages: Provides machine thread in wood, reusable, neat.
- Limitations: Requires correct hole; can pull out under high load.
- Applications: Furniture, speaker cabinets, fixtures.
- Select when: Threaded fastening into wood/panel.

### Flat washer
- Description: Thin annular disc to distribute bolt load, protect surface, provide bearing surface; standards ISO 7089/7090, DIN 125 — [Fastener Technical Manual](https://classccomponents.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Fastener-Technical-Manual.pdf); [ANSI washer standards](https://webstore.ansi.org/industry/fasteners/washers).
- Load capacity: Increases bearing area; reduces surface crushing.
- Advantages: Protects parts, improves load distribution, cheap.
- Limitations: Can relax; not a lock by itself.
- Applications: All bolted joints.
- Select when: Soft materials, large holes, surface protection.

### Spring (helical) lock washer
- Description: Split helical spring washer that applies spring force and slight rotation resistance under load — [Fastener Technical Manual (BS4464)](https://classccomponents.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Fastener-Technical-Manual.pdf).
- Load capacity: Light locking; not a primary load carrier.
- Advantages: Simple, cheap, some vibration resistance.
- Limitations: Effectiveness debated; can damage surfaces; not for critical joints.
- Applications: General machinery, light vibration.
- Select when: Low-cost vibration resistance (non-critical).

### Toothed lock washer (internal/external star)
- Description: Washer with radial teeth that bite into mating surfaces to resist rotation — [ANSI B18.22.1](https://webstore.ansi.org/industry/fasteners/washers) (per search result).
- Load capacity: Locking only; low load.
- Advantages: Good locking in soft materials, conductive grounding.
- Limitations: Damages surfaces; single use-ish; not for high preload.
- Applications: Electrical grounding, sheet metal, small assemblies.
- Select when: Need positive locking, soft substrates.

### Solid rivet
- Description: Smooth shank with factory head; tail bucked into shop head; permanent — [Wikipedia, Rivet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rivet).
- Load capacity: Excellent shear; moderate tension; fatigue-resistant in aircraft.
- Advantages: Reliable, high shear, no threads, low cost.
- Limitations: Requires access both sides; skilled install; removal destructive.
- Applications: Aircraft, bridges, boilers, sheet metal.
- Select when: Permanent shear joint, both-side access, high reliability.

### Blind rivet (pop rivet)
- Description: Tubular rivet with mandrel; installed from one side; mandrel breaks after expansion — [Wikipedia, Rivet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rivet).
- Load capacity: Light-to-moderate shear/tension; structural versions higher.
- Advantages: One-side access, fast, simple tools.
- Limitations: Lower strength than solid; mandrel may loosen; hole size critical.
- Applications: Sheet metal, enclosures, automotive panels.
- Select when: One-side access, moderate loads, speed.

### Semi-tubular rivet
- Description: Rivet with partial hole at tail; rolled over with ~1/4 force of solid — [Wikipedia, Rivet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rivet).
- Load capacity: Moderate; good for pivots.
- Advantages: Low installation force, good for moving joints.
- Limitations: Less strength than solid; requires tooling.
- Applications: Hinges, ladders, HVAC, electronics.
- Select when: Pivot points, high-volume production.

### Dowel pin
- Description: Precision ground solid cylinder pressed into reamed hole for alignment; hardened steel — [Albany County Fasteners guide](https://www.albanycountyfasteners.com/fasteners-101-pins-clips-information); [Ideambox](https://ideambox.com/assets/downloads/retaining-rings-pins.pdf).
- Load capacity: Location/shear; not for clamping tension.
- Advantages: Precise alignment, repeatable, cheap.
- Limitations: Requires reamed hole; over-constraint risk; not tension.
- Applications: Jigs, fixtures, dies, molds, machinery alignment.
- Select when: Precise location, two dowels + bolts for clamping.

### Clevis pin
- Description: Smooth cylindrical pin with head and cross-hole for cotter pin; pivot — [Albany County Fasteners](https://www.albanycountyfasteners.com/fasteners-101-pins-clips-information); [RS Online](https://uk.rs-online.com/web/c/fasteners-fixings/pins-keys-retaining-clips/).
- Load capacity: Moderate shear in pivoting joints.
- Advantages: Removable pivot, easy service.
- Limitations: Requires cotter pin; clearance fit.
- Applications: Linkages, yokes, trailers, agricultural equipment.
- Select when: Serviceable pivot connection.

### Cotter pin (split pin)
- Description: Split pin inserted through hole and legs bent to lock — [Albany County Fasteners](https://www.albanycountyfasteners.com/fasteners-101-pins-clips-information).
- Load capacity: Light retention/shear locking only.
- Advantages: Cheap, easy, removable.
- Limitations: One-time-ish; low strength; can snag.
- Applications: Secure clevis pins, castle nuts, linkages.
- Select when: Locking pins/nuts, light duty.

### Taper pin
- Description: Conical pin (1:48 or 1/4" per ft taper) pressed into tapered reamed hole — [Albany County Fasteners installation guide](https://www.albanycountyfasteners.com/media/d1/05/29/1764613447/pin-clip-installation-guide.pdf?ts=1764613447).
- Load capacity: Location + light torque/shear.
- Advantages: Self-centering, precise, removable with drift.
- Limitations: Requires tapered reamer; cost.
- Applications: Collars, hubs, levers on shafts.
- Select when: Precise location with light torque, removable.

### Spring (roll) pin (slotted/coiled)
- Description: Hollow slotted or coiled spring steel pin that grips drilled hole by spring force — [Albany County Fasteners](https://www.albanycountyfasteners.com/fasteners-101-pins-clips-information); [RS Online](https://uk.rs-online.com/web/c/fasteners-fixings/pins-keys-retaining-clips/).
- Load capacity: Location + light-to-moderate torque/shear.
- Advantages: Self-retaining, cheap, no reaming, absorbs hole tolerance.
- Limitations: Lower precision than dowel; can loosen in oversized holes.
- Applications: General machinery, hinges, low-cost assemblies.
- Select when: Forgiving holes, light torque, low cost.

### Grooved pin
- Description: Solid pin with longitudinal grooves that compress to grip drilled hole — [Albany County Fasteners installation guide](https://www.albanycountyfasteners.com/media/d1/05/29/1764613447/pin-clip-installation-guide.pdf?ts=1764613447).
- Load capacity: Press-retained location/shear.
- Advantages: Easier than solid dowel, no reaming, good retention.
- Limitations: Less precise than dowel.
- Applications: Levers, hinges, machinery.
- Select when: Press retention in drilled holes.

### Square key
- Description: Square cross-section key seated in shaft/keyway transmitting torque — [Engineering Hardware catalog](https://engineeringhardware.com/); [Cromwell](https://www.cromwell.co.uk/shop/fasteners-and-fixings/rivets-pins-and-keys/c/2342).
- Load capacity: Torque transmission; key shears as weak link (protects drivetrain).
- Advantages: Standard, high torque, simple.
- Limitations: Stress concentration; loosening possible; not for high speed.
- Applications: Gears, pulleys, couplings on shafts.
- Select when: Torque transmission, sacrificial weak link desired.

### Flat (parallel) key
- Description: Rectangular key (width > height) in shaft/keyway; common metric standard DIN 6885 — [Engineering Hardware](https://engineeringhardware.com/).
- Load capacity: Torque transmission; lower height than square.
- Advantages: Standard, fits shallow keyways, common.
- Limitations: Less torque than square for same width? (unverified) stress concentration.
- Applications: Hubs, gears, sprockets.
- Select when: Standard shaft-hub torque (metric).

### Woodruff key
- Description: Semicircular (half-moon) key that seats in curved keyseat; self-aligning — [Engineering Hardware](https://engineeringhardware.com/); [Cromwell](https://www.cromwell.co.uk/shop/fasteners-and-fixings/rivets-pins-and-keys/c/2342).
- Load capacity: Moderate torque; good in tapered shafts.
- Advantages: Self-aligning, stays in shaft during assembly, good for tapers.
- Limitations: Weaker than square key for same size; keyseat weakens shaft.
- Applications: Tapered shafts, automotive, machine tools.
- Select when: Tapered shafts, easy assembly, moderate torque.

### Gib-head taper key
- Description: Tapered key with gib head for extraction; used for heavy-duty hubs — [Engineering Hardware](https://engineeringhardware.com/).
- Load capacity: High torque; removable.
- Advantages: Easy removal, high torque, adjustable fit.
- Limitations: Requires tapered keyway; cost.
- Applications: Large hubs, couplings, heavy machinery.
- Select when: Heavy torque, need removable key.

### Retaining ring (internal/external circlip)
- Description: Spring steel ring seated in groove on shaft (external) or bore (internal); axial retention — [Albany County Fasteners](https://www.albanycountyfasteners.com/fasteners-101-pins-clips-information); [Ideambox](https://ideambox.com/assets/downloads/retaining-rings-pins.pdf); DIN 471/472.
- Load capacity: Limited thrust; ring can dish/pop out if overloaded.
- Advantages: Fast, cheap, reusable, no threads.
- Limitations: Groove required; limited thrust; wear.
- Applications: Bearings, gears, pulleys on shafts.
- Select when: Axial retention in grooved shaft/bore.

### E-clip (external snap ring)
- Description: E-shaped ring snaps onto shaft/groove from side; no pliers needed — [Albany County Fasteners](https://www.albanycountyfasteners.com/fasteners-101-pins-clips-information).
- Load capacity: Light thrust.
- Advantages: Fast, one-side install, no tools.
- Limitations: Lower capacity than grooved rings.
- Applications: Light-duty shaft retention, knobs, wheels.
- Select when: Light loads, fast assembly.

### Spiral retaining ring
- Description: Multi-turn constant-section ring with 360° contact; no lugs — [Ideambox](https://ideambox.com/assets/downloads/retaining-rings-pins.pdf).
- Load capacity: Higher thrust than standard rings; clean OD.
- Advantages: Higher thrust, 360° contact, smooth.
- Limitations: Cost, special install.
- Applications: High-thrust retention, sealed assemblies.
- Select when: Higher thrust than standard circlip.

### Push-on retainer (starlock)
- Description: Toothed washer pushed onto shaft; one-time use — [Albany County Fasteners](https://www.albanycountyfasteners.com/fasteners-101-pins-clips-information).
- Load capacity: Light-to-medium.
- Advantages: No groove, fast, cheap.
- Limitations: One-time use, damages shaft, low capacity.
- Applications: Knobs, handles, wheels, production.
- Select when: One-time assembly, no groove, low cost.

### Set screw
- Description: Headless screw threaded into part to clamp/lock another part (shaft in hub); point styles flat/cone/cup/dog — [Fastener Technical Manual (DIN 913-916/ISO 4026-4029)](https://classccomponents.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Fastener-Technical-Manual.pdf).
- Load capacity: Clamping friction; not for high torque alone.
- Advantages: Compact, flush, adjustable.
- Limitations: Can damage shaft, loosens under vibration, low torque capacity alone.
- Applications: Collars, gears on shafts, adjustable components.
- Select when: Positioning/locking, light-to-moderate torque (with key for high torque).

### Threaded insert (helicoil / rivet nut)
- Description: Internal thread repair/anchor inserted into parent material; helicoil wire coil or rivet-nut expands — [UF fasteners lab notes](https://web.mae.ufl.edu/designlab/lab%20assignments/eml2322l-fasteners.pdf) (rivet nuts) and general knowledge (flagged unverified specifics).
- Load capacity: Restores/creates thread strength in soft materials.
- Advantages: Repairs stripped threads, strong threads in aluminum/plastic.
- Limitations: Installation tooling; cost; can pull out if misinstalled.
- Applications: Thread repair, electronics, aerospace, soft metals.
- Select when: Repair or threaded hole in soft material.

## Joints

### Takeaway
Joint selection trades permanence, load path, and manufacturability: welds give full-strength permanent metal joints; bolts give serviceable high-strength joints; rivets/pins give permanent shear/location; adhesives give large-area shear bonding; integral wood joints give alignment/strength — [KEVOS](https://kevos.com/insights/choosing-a-joining-method/); [Ideambox welded/adhesive joints](https://ideambox.com/assets/downloads/welded-adhesive-joints.pdf); [BBC Bitesize wood joints](https://www.bbc.co.uk/bitesize/articles/zn4g6rd).

### Cited Findings
- Welding fuses parts into one; full-penetration butt weld can match plate strength; fillet welds carry load through throat (~0.7×leg) — [KEVOS](https://kevos.com/insights/choosing-a-joining-method/); [Ideambox](https://ideambox.com/assets/downloads/welded-adhesive-joints.pdf).
- Adhesive joints strong in shear, weak in peel/cleavage; need surface prep and overlap — [Ideambox](https://ideambox.com/assets/downloads/welded-adhesive-joints.pdf); [KEVOS](https://kevos.com/insights/choosing-a-joining-method/).
- Riveting does not loosen, joins thin sheet/dissimilar metals; permanent — [KEVOS](https://kevos.com/insights/choosing-a-joining-method/).
- Bolting: clamping/preload or shank shear; dismantlable, joins dissimilar materials, no heat — [KEVOS](https://kevos.com/insights/choosing-a-joining-method/).
- Wood joints (mortise-and-tenon, dovetail, dowelled etc.) chosen by strength/aesthetics — [BBC Bitesize](https://www.bbc.co.uk/bitesize/articles/zn4g6rd).

### Inferences
- Hybrid joints (adhesive + rivet/spot weld) combine load spreading with peel resistance — [KEVOS](https://kevos.com/insights/choosing-a-joining-method/).

### Gaps
- Quantitative strength values for wood joints and snap-fits not found in sources; flagged unverified.

### Bolted joint
- Description: Threaded fastener clamps parts; load by friction (preload) or shank shear/bearing — [Wikipedia, Bolt (fastener)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolt_(fastener)); [KEVOS](https://kevos.com/insights/choosing-a-joining-method/).
- Load capacity: High tension/shear depending on bolt grade and preload; slip-critical uses friction, bearing uses bolt shear — [Wikipedia, Bolt (fastener)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolt_(fastener)).
- Advantages: Removable, high strength, joins dissimilar materials, no heat.
- Limitations: Needs holes/access, can loosen, preload control needed.
- Applications: Machinery, structures, pressure vessels, automotive.
- Select when: Serviceable, high load, dissimilar materials.

### Welded joint – fillet
- Description: Triangular bead in corner (T/lap/corner); most common; fails across throat a=0.707×leg — [Ideambox](https://ideambox.com/assets/downloads/welded-adhesive-joints.pdf); [KEVOS](https://kevos.com/insights/choosing-a-joining-method/).
- Load capacity: Moderate; throat-limited; fatigue-sensitive at toe.
- Advantages: No edge prep, fast, versatile.
- Limitations: Distortion, HAZ, fatigue at toe, similar metals only.
- Applications: Fabrication, frames, brackets.
- Select when: General fabrication, corner/lap joints, moderate loads.

### Welded joint – butt (full penetration)
- Description: Edges butted and fused through full thickness; force passes straight through — [Ideambox](https://ideambox.com/assets/downloads/welded-adhesive-joints.pdf); [KEVOS](https://kevos.com/insights/choosing-a-joining-method/).
- Load capacity: Highest; can equal base metal strength.
- Advantages: Full strength, smooth flush profile.
- Limitations: Edge prep, access to root, distortion, cost.
- Applications: Pressure vessels, pipelines, structural members.
- Select when: Full strength, flush joint, compatible metals.

### Spot weld
- Description: Resistance weld at discrete points joining sheet; used in automotive bodies — [KEVOS](https://kevos.com/insights/choosing-a-joining-method/) (hybrid weld-bonding); [Ideambox](https://ideambox.com/assets/downloads/welded-adhesive-joints.pdf).
- Load capacity: Moderate shear per spot; many spots share load.
- Advantages: Fast, automatable, no consumables, thin sheet.
- Limitations: Local stress concentration, fatigue, access for electrodes.
- Applications: Automotive body-in-white, sheet metal assemblies.
- Select when: High-volume thin sheet, automated production.

### Seam weld
- Description: Continuous or overlapping spot welds along seam; can be watertight — [Fronius blog](https://blog.fronius.com/welding/en/know-how/types-of-welding-joints-and-seams/) (seam types).
- Load capacity: Moderate; leak-tight seam.
- Advantages: Sealed joints, continuous strength.
- Limitations: Distortion, slower than spot, sheet thickness limits.
- Applications: Tanks, pipes, containers.
- Select when: Leak-tight sheet joints.

### Riveted joint
- Description: Permanent fastener in shear; plates in bearing; does not loosen — [KEVOS](https://kevos.com/insights/choosing-a-joining-method/); [Wikipedia, Rivet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rivet).
- Load capacity: Shear/bearing; limited tension.
- Advantages: Permanent, vibration-resistant, dissimilar metals, thin sheet.
- Limitations: Needs holes, removal destructive, labor-intensive.
- Applications: Aircraft, bridges (legacy), sheet metal.
- Select when: Permanent shear joint, vibration, thin sheet.

### Pinned joint
- Description: Pin in shear for location/torque; dowel for precision, spring pin for forgiving holes — [Ideambox](https://ideambox.com/assets/downloads/retaining-rings-pins.pdf).
- Load capacity: Shear; pins carry shear not tension.
- Advantages: Simple, cheap, alignment.
- Limitations: Not for clamping; over-constraint.
- Applications: Hinges, linkages, locating features.
- Select when: Location/shear, removable pivot.

### Keyed joint (shaft-hub)
- Description: Key in keyway transmits torque between shaft and hub — [Engineering Hardware](https://engineeringhardware.com/); [Cromwell](https://www.cromwell.co.uk/shop/fasteners-and-fixings/rivets-pins-and-keys/c/2342).
- Load capacity: Torque; key is shear weak link.
- Advantages: Standard, high torque, simple.
- Limitations: Stress concentration, loosening, speed limits.
- Applications: Gears, pulleys, couplings.
- Select when: Torque transmission, sacrificial failure.

### Press-fit / interference fit
- Description: Shaft pressed into undersized hole; retention by friction/interference — [Covestro design guide](https://solutions.covestro.com/-/media/covestro/solution-center/brands/downloads/imported/1556890495.pdf) (press fits); [KEVOS mechanical forming](https://kevos.com/insights/choosing-a-joining-method/).
- Load capacity: Torque/axial retention by friction; depends on interference and materials.
- Advantages: No fasteners, fast, clean.
- Limitations: Assembly force, hoop stress, disassembly difficult, tolerance-sensitive.
- Applications: Bearings on shafts, gears, bushings.
- Select when: Permanent/semi-permanent fit, no fastener access.

### Adhesive bonded joint
- Description: Load transferred through thin adhesive layer over overlap; strong in shear, weak in peel — [Ideambox](https://ideambox.com/assets/downloads/welded-adhesive-joints.pdf); [KEVOS](https://kevos.com/insights/choosing-a-joining-method/).
- Load capacity: Shear across bonded area; e.g. 25×12.5 mm overlap at 15 MPa ≈ 4.7 kN — [KEVOS](https://kevos.com/insights/choosing-a-joining-method/).
- Advantages: No holes, spreads load, seals, mixed materials.
- Limitations: Surface prep critical, cure time, temperature limits, weak peel.
- Applications: Automotive bodies, aircraft panels, plastics/composites.
- Select when: Thin/dissimilar materials, sealed/smooth joints.

### Snap-fit joint
- Description: Integral flexible features interlock during assembly; common in plastics — [Covestro design guide](https://solutions.covestro.com/-/media/covestro/solution-center/brands/downloads/imported/1556890495.pdf).
- Load capacity: Light-to-moderate; depends on material strain.
- Advantages: No fasteners, fast, cheap, recyclable.
- Limitations: Plastic material limits, fatigue, one-time vs repeated.
- Applications: Consumer products, enclosures, plastic assemblies.
- Select when: High-volume plastic parts, light loads.

### Dovetail joint
- Description: Interlocking trapezoidal pins/tails; strong mechanical joint in wood — [BBC Bitesize](https://www.bbc.co.uk/bitesize/articles/zn4g6rd).
- Load capacity: High resistance to pull-out; good strength.
- Advantages: Strong, attractive, no fasteners.
- Limitations: Difficult to cut, skill required.
- Applications: Drawers, boxes, furniture.
- Select when: Wood furniture, pull-out loads, aesthetics.

### Mortise-and-tenon joint
- Description: Tenon inserted into mortise; strong wood joint — [BBC Bitesize](https://www.bbc.co.uk/bitesize/articles/zn4g6rd).
- Load capacity: High; commonly for legs/frames.
- Advantages: Strong, traditional, good load transfer.
- Limitations: Time-consuming, visible if not hidden.
- Applications: Chairs, tables, timber frames.
- Select when: Structural wood frames.

### Tongue-and-groove joint
- Description: Tongue on one board fits groove on another for alignment and strength — general woodworking knowledge (unverified by cited sources; flag).
- Load capacity: Moderate; alignment primarily.
- Advantages: Self-aligning, increases glue surface, neat.
- Limitations: Weak if not glued; machining needed.
- Applications: Flooring, paneling, cabinetry.
- Select when: Panels/flooring alignment.

## Bearings

### Takeaway
Bearings constrain motion and reduce friction; rolling-element bearings suit most rotary loads, plain bearings suit low cost/high load, fluid/magnetic/air/flexure suit extreme speed/precision, with life governed by ISO 281 L10 for rolling bearings — [Wikipedia, Bearing (mechanical)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bearing_(mechanical)); [Wikipedia, Rolling-element bearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling-element_bearing).

### Cited Findings
- Plain bearing: rubbing surfaces, cheapest, compact, high load capacity — [Wikipedia, Plain bearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plain_bearing).
- Rolling-element bearing: balls/rollers; friction ~0.005; L10=(C/P)^p (p=3 ball, 4 line contact, 3.33 roller) — [Wikipedia, Rolling-element bearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling-element_bearing).
- Magnetic bearing: levitation, no wear, highest speeds, needs power/control and backup — [Wikipedia, Magnetic bearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_bearing).
- Bearing material trade-offs (steel, stainless, ceramic, bronze, plastics) — [Wikipedia, Bearing (mechanical)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bearing_(mechanical)).

### Inferences
- For most industrial rotary applications, deep-groove ball bearings are default; tapered rollers for combined radial/axial vehicle loads; plain bearings for low speed/high load or cost.

### Gaps
- Air bearing and flexure bearing details only from summary table; deeper specs not sourced here.

---

### Deep groove ball bearing
- Description: Single-row ball bearing with deep race grooves; handles radial and moderate axial loads both directions — [Wikipedia, Rolling-element bearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling-element_bearing).
- Load capacity: Moderate radial/axial; high speed.
- Advantages: Versatile, cheap, high speed, widely available.
- Limitations: Lower load than rollers; axial limited.
- Applications: Electric motors, pumps, general machinery.
- Select when: General-purpose rotary, moderate loads, high speed.

### Angular contact ball bearing
- Description: Ball bearing with contact angle; supports combined radial and one-direction axial (or paired for both) — [Wikipedia, Rolling-element bearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling-element_bearing) (type 6).
- Load capacity: High axial + radial; precision.
- Advantages: High speed, axial capacity, precision.
- Limitations: Needs preload/pairing for bidirectional axial.
- Applications: Spindles, pumps, gearboxes.
- Select when: Combined loads, precision, high speed.

### Thrust ball bearing
- Description: Flat washers with balls; supports axial (thrust) loads only — [Wikipedia, Rolling-element bearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling-element_bearing) (thrust loadings).
- Load capacity: Axial only; no radial.
- Advantages: Simple, low friction axial support.
- Limitations: No radial capacity; speed limits.
- Applications: Vertical shafts, clutches.
- Select when: Pure axial load.

### Cylindrical roller bearing
- Description: Cylindrical rollers between races; high radial capacity, low axial — [Wikipedia, Rolling-element bearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling-element_bearing).
- Load capacity: High radial; low axial; sensitive to misalignment.
- Advantages: High radial load, high speed (some).
- Limitations: Poor misalignment tolerance, low axial.
- Applications: Gearboxes, electric motors, heavy machinery.
- Select when: High radial load, good alignment.

### Tapered roller bearing
- Description: Conical rollers/races support both radial and axial; common in vehicles — [Wikipedia, Rolling-element bearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling-element_bearing).
- Load capacity: High combined radial+axial.
- Advantages: High load, handles cornering loads.
- Limitations: More expensive, friction from collar, needs preload.
- Applications: Wheel hubs, gearboxes, differentials.
- Select when: Combined loads, automotive, heavy.

### Needle roller bearing
- Description: Long thin rollers; small OD, high radial capacity in limited space — [Wikipedia, Rolling-element bearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling-element_bearing).
- Load capacity: High radial in compact space; fatigues faster.
- Advantages: Compact, high load density.
- Limitations: Shorter fatigue life, sensitive to contamination.
- Applications: Transmissions, rocker arms, compact mechanisms.
- Select when: Space-constrained high radial load.

### Spherical roller bearing
- Description: Barrel rollers in spherical outer race; accommodates misalignment — [Wikipedia, Rolling-element bearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling-element_bearing).
- Load capacity: High radial + moderate axial; misalignment-tolerant.
- Advantages: Misalignment compensation, heavy load.
- Limitations: Higher friction/cost, sliding.
- Applications: Heavy industry, conveyors, wind turbines.
- Select when: Heavy load with misalignment.

### CARB toroidal roller bearing
- Description: Toroidal rollers; accommodates misalignment and axial displacement; non-locating — [Wikipedia, Rolling-element bearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling-element_bearing); SKF 1995.
- Load capacity: High radial; no axial location.
- Advantages: Thermal expansion accommodation, misalignment.
- Limitations: Non-locating; must pair with locating bearing.
- Applications: Long shafts, paper machines, thermal expansion.
- Select when: Non-locating bearing paired with locating.

### Plain / sleeve bearing (journal)
- Description: Shaft slides in hole/bushing; no rolling elements; cheapest — [Wikipedia, Plain bearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plain_bearing).
- Load capacity: High (P/PV limited by material); low speed typical.
- Advantages: Cheap, compact, high load, tolerant of contamination.
- Limitations: Higher friction, wear, lubrication needed.
- Applications: Engines, pumps, low-speed/high-load.
- Select when: Low cost, high load, low speed, dirty environments.

### Hydrodynamic (journal) bearing
- Description: Fluid film separates surfaces via shaft rotation; full-film lubrication — [Wikipedia, Plain bearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plain_bearing) (fluid lubrication).
- Load capacity: Very high; limited by oil whirl/whip.
- Advantages: Very high load, low friction at speed, long life.
- Limitations: Needs rotation to lift; oil whirl risk; start/stop wear.
- Applications: Turbomachinery, large engines, turbines.
- Select when: High speed, high load, continuous operation.

### Hydrostatic bearing
- Description: Externally pumped fluid film; supports load at zero speed — [Wikipedia, Plain bearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plain_bearing) (fluid lubrication).
- Load capacity: Very high; stiffness high.
- Advantages: Zero friction at zero speed, very stiff, high load.
- Limitations: Pump/complexity, contamination sensitivity, cost.
- Applications: Precision machines, heavy spindles, CMMs.
- Select when: Precision, heavy load, low speed/start-stop.

### Magnetic bearing
- Description: Magnetic levitation; no contact; highest speeds — [Wikipedia, Magnetic bearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_bearing).
- Load capacity: Moderate (active); no wear; speed unlimited by bearing.
- Advantages: No friction/wear, oil-free, high speed, vibration control.
- Limitations: Power/control required, backup bearing, cost, size.
- Applications: Turbomachinery, flywheels, vacuum pumps, artificial hearts.
- Select when: Oil-free, ultra-high speed, vacuum, long life.

### Air bearing
- Description: Gas (air) film separates surfaces; non-contact; very low friction — [Wikipedia, Bearing (mechanical)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bearing_(mechanical)) (fluid bearing).
- Load capacity: Low-to-moderate; high stiffness.
- Advantages: Zero friction, ultra-precision, clean.
- Limitations: Low load, contamination sensitivity, air supply.
- Applications: CMMs, precision spindles, lithography.
- Select when: Ultra-precision, clean, light load.

### Flexure bearing
- Description: Elastic deformation provides motion; no contact friction — [Wikipedia, Bearing (mechanical)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bearing_(mechanical)).
- Load capacity: Low; limited range.
- Advantages: No backlash, ultra-smooth, maintenance-free.
- Limitations: Limited travel, fatigue life depends on strain.
- Applications: Optical alignment, precision instruments.
- Select when: Ultra-precision, small motion, no maintenance.

### Jewel bearing
- Description: Sapphire/ruby plain bearing for low friction in instruments — [Wikipedia, Plain bearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plain_bearing) (jewels).
- Load capacity: Very low.
- Advantages: Very low friction, precise, small.
- Limitations: Brittle, low load, cost.
- Applications: Watches, instruments.
- Select when: Tiny, low-load, high precision.
