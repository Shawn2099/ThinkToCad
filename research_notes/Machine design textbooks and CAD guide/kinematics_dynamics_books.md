# Kinematics and Dynamics of Machinery: Authoritative Textbooks

Pointer/guide for an AI design assistant. Compiled from publisher pages, Library of Congress records, Google Books, and secondary textbook summaries cited inline. Scope: kinematic analysis and synthesis of mechanisms, cams, gears, gear trains, balancing, vibrations, and dynamics of machinery.

## Which textbooks cover kinematics and dynamics of machinery, and what does each own?

### Takeaway
The core canon splits into two families: (1) kinematics-first, synthesis-heavy texts (Norton "Design of Machinery"; Erdman & Sandor "Mechanism Design" vols 1–2; McCarthy & Soh "Geometric Design of Linkages") and (2) mechanisms-and-machines texts that pair kinematics with dynamics, cams, gears, balancing, vibrations, and engines (Shigley/Uicker/Pennock; Mabie & Reinholtz; Rao & Dukkipati; Ghosh & Mallik). Norton is the default undergraduate synthesis/analysis reference; Uicker/Pennock is the most exhaustive kinematics reference; Mabie & Reinholtz is the classic force-analysis/balancing reference; Erdman & Sandor vol 2 is the graduate synthesis/curvature/balancing reference; McCarthy & Soh is the advanced geometric (algebraic) synthesis reference.

### Inferences
- For an AI design assistant, the most useful primary references are Norton (undergraduate synthesis + dynamics, with software appendices), Uicker/Pennock (exhaustive kinematics + gear families + spatial/robotics), Mabie & Reinholtz (gear AGMA standards, force analysis, balancing), Erdman & Sandor vol.2 (advanced synthesis, curvature, elastodynamics, balancing), and McCarthy & Soh (algebraic synthesis for planar/spherical/spatial). Norton's *Cam Design and Manufacturing Handbook* is the specialized cam reference.
- When a single fact is needed (e.g., minimum teeth to avoid undercutting ≈ 2/sin²φ), prefer the mechanism notes already compiled (cams_and_gears.md) and use these books only for derivation/procedure context.

## Topic-to-book map: which book to consult for which topic/equations?

### Takeaway
Map topics to the text that "owns" them: kinematic analysis → Uicker/Pennock (most exhaustive) or Norton; synthesis (graphical/analytical 2–3 position) → Norton/Erdman vol.1; advanced synthesis (4–5 position, Burmester, curvature) → Erdman vol.2; algebraic/spherical/spatial synthesis → McCarthy & Soh; cams (design + dynamics) → Norton ch.8/15 + Norton Cam Handbook; gears (families + AGMA) → Mabie ch.4–7 or Uicker ch.6–10; balancing → Uicker ch.19 / Mabie ch.10 / Erdman vol.2 ch.5; vibrations → Uicker ch.17 / Ghosh ch.11 / Rao; engine dynamics → Norton ch.13–14 / Ghosh ch.5; spatial/robotics → Uicker ch.12–13 / Erdman vol.2 ch.6 / McCarthy.

### Inferences
- For a quick mechanism-family decision (four-bar, slider-crank, planetary), Norton ch.2–3 + Uicker ch.1 give mobility/Grashof/MA. For exact synthesis with 4–5 positions, use Erdman vol.2 ch.3 or McCarthy & Soh. For gear sizing, use Mabie ch.4–7 or Uicker ch.6–10 plus AGMA/ISO standards. For balancing a rotor or linkage, use Erdman vol.2 ch.5 or Uicker ch.19. For vibration/critical speed, use Uicker ch.17 or Rao ch.18–19.

### Gaps
- No single text covers both advanced algebraic synthesis (McCarthy) and machine-element rating (AGMA/ISO); combine texts + standards. — [inference from TOCs]

## Practical guidance for an AI design assistant

### Takeaway
Default to Norton for undergraduate-style analysis/synthesis and dynamics; escalate to Uicker/Pennock for exhaustive kinematics or gear-family detail; to Mabie for AGMA gear standards and force analysis; to Erdman vol.2 for advanced synthesis, curvature, elastodynamics, and linkage balancing; to McCarthy & Soh for algebraic/spherical/spatial synthesis; to Norton's Cam Handbook for cam design/manufacturing. Always pair with standards (AGMA/ISO) for rated designs.

### Cited Findings
- Norton emphasizes "synthesis and design" with "modern tools needed for analysis of the kinematics and dynamics of machinery" and ships software appendices (computer programs) — useful for an assistant generating code. — [McGraw Hill](https://www.mheducation.com/highered/product/design-of-machinery-norton.html)
- Erdman & Sandor emphasize CAD/CAM/CAE and interactive graphics; vol.2 includes LINCAGES package and computer programs — useful for synthesis automation. — [pdfcoffee vol.2 preface](https://pdfcoffee.com/sandor-arthur-g-erdman-advanced-mechanism-design-analysis-and-synthesis-vol-ii-1984-pdf-free.html)
- Uicker/Pennock covers "displacements, velocities, accelerations, and static and dynamic forces required" with economical coverage of many topics — good breadth reference. — [OUP](https://global.oup.com/ukhe/product/theory-of-machines-and-mechanisms-9780190264505)
- Mabie's 4th ed. includes "latest AGMA standards for U.S. and metric gears" and BASIC/FORTRAN programs — useful for gear sizing and code. — [Wiley](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Mechanisms+and+Dynamics+of+Machinery%2C+4th+Edition-p-9780471802372)
- McCarthy & Soh provides "mathematical theory of design for articulated mechanical systems" with constraint equations and solution strategies — useful for algebraic synthesis code. — [Springer](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-1-4419-7892-9)

### Inferences
- A practical assistant workflow: (1) classify motion requirement; (2) pick mechanism family using Norton/Uicker; (3) size using standard modules/DP and AGMA/ISO via Mabie; (4) synthesize dimensions using Norton/Erdman/McCarthy; (5) verify kinematics (velocity/acceleration) with Uicker/Norton; (6) check dynamics/balancing/vibration with Uicker/Mabie/Erdman; (7) for cams, use Norton ch.8/15 + Cam Handbook; (8) prototype and measure.
