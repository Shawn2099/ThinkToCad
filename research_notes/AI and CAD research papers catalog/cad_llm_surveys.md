# Survey/Review Papers on AI/LLM for CAD

Scope: comprehensive catalog of survey/review/overview papers on AI/LLM applications in CAD, 2024-2026. Notes compiled from web search (arXiv, Google Scholar, publisher pages). Items not fully verified are flagged.

## Key Question 1: What survey/review papers exist on AI for CAD (2024-2026)?

### Takeaway
A cluster of ~12-15 survey/review/overview papers appeared 2024-2026 covering LLM-for-CAD, geometric deep learning for CAD, AI-driven 3D CAD generation, generative AI for engineering-grade parametric CAD, intelligent CAD, multimodal LLMs in parametric CAD, and LLMs in design/manufacturing. The most comprehensive LLM-specific survey is Zhang et al. (arXiv 2505.08137, ACM CSUR 2026).

- Coverage skews toward mechanical/product design; shipbuilding/textile noted as underexplored (Zhang et al.).

## Key Question 2: What topics do they cover?

### Takeaway
Common topic clusters: text-to-CAD / natural-language-driven CAD; CAD code/program generation (CadQuery, Python, JSON command sequences); parametric CAD generation & editing; assembly modeling; manufacturing feature recognition / CAPP / DfM; multimodal inputs (text+image+sketch); evaluation metrics/benchmarks; datasets.

### Cited Findings
- Zhang et al. taxonomy of six application areas: Data Generation, CAD Code Generation, Parametric CAD Generation, Image Generation, Model Evaluation, Text Generation. — [arXiv 2505.08137](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.08137)
- Zhang et al. report majority of works focus on CAD code generation (27 works), then text generation (24), parametric CAD generation (20); most use intermediate representations (Python scripts, JSON) rather than direct 3D output. — [arXiv 2505.08137](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.08137)
- Wu et al. survey covers AI for 3D CAD model generation across representations (point clouds, meshes, surfaces, parametric) and conditions (text/image/sketch/point cloud), traditional ML→LLM. — [SciOpen](https://www.sciopen.com/article/10.26599/CVM.2025.9450521)
- Heidari & Iosifidis covers similarity analysis & retrieval, 2D/3D CAD model synthesis, CAD from point clouds and single/multi-view images, datasets, open-source code. — [arXiv 2402.17695](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.17695)
- Zhou & Camba covers 2D sketching automation, 3D model generation, design optimization, natural-language interface development for parametric CAD. — [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S095741742501142X)
- Generative AI & LLMs for CAD Assembly Design & MFR review covers text-to-CAD, graph transformers, B-Rep feature recognition, CAPP, assembly mating constraints. — [DOI 10.52783/cana.v32.7270](https://doi.org/10.52783/cana.v32.7270)
- Makatura et al. (MIT) covers design generation, design spaces/variations, design-for-manufacturing, performance evaluation, inverse design/search. — [MIT](https://doi.org/10.21428/e4baedd9.745b62fa)
- Tan et al. (Chinese JCAD review) covers datasets, CAD representation, forward engineering, reverse engineering, LLM for CAD, future trends (dataset quality, precision/scale, LLM deep integration, CAD-CAE co-optimization). — [JCAD](https://www.jcad.cn/cn/article/pdf/preview/10.3724/SP.J.1089.2025-00241.pdf)
- Klein et al. (IEEE CG&A) covers generative AI for 3D CAD in engineering design process, with design theory & human cognition perspectives. — [IEEE CSDL](https://www.computer.org/csdl/magazine/cg/2026/03/11397452/2e9R0ceuB68)
- Critical review (Machines 2026) covers constrained sketches, B-Reps, parametric sequences, executable CAD programs, LLMs/multimodal models, CAD agents, commercial-CAD integration, CAD-CAE. — [MDPI Machines](https://doi.org/10.3390/machines14101206)
- Mustapha (AEI 2024) covers LLMs in mechanics, product design, manufacturing (broader than CAD). — [AEI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aei.2024.103066)
- Intelligent CAD 2.0 (Zou et al.) covers AI roles across design process, early-stage design, ICAD 1.0 vs 2.0 framework. — [arXiv 2410.03759](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.03759)

### Inferences
- "Editing" is emerging as distinct topic (CADMorph, geometry-driven parametric editing) but not a major cluster in surveys yet; most surveys treat editing under parametric generation.
- Assembly/MFR/CAPP covered by niche reviews, not central to LLM-CAD surveys.

### Gaps
- Few surveys dedicate sections to CAD editing, assembly constraints, or manufacturing (DfM) beyond brief mentions; assembly-specific survey gap noted.

## Key Question 3: Key trends and open challenges

### Takeaway
Trends: intermediate code/JSON representations instead of direct 3D; multimodal LLMs; GPT-4/GPT-4o dominance; evaluation via Chamfer Distance/invalid rate; agentic multi-agent systems; growing datasets (DeepCAD, Fusion360 Gallery, ABC). Challenges: data scarcity/quality, proprietary formats, mixed data types, limited operation coverage (sketch+extrude only), geometric hallucination, topological validity, lack of engineering-grade/editability benchmarks, scalability/interoperability/security.

### Cited Findings
- Trend: nearly all approaches use LLMs to generate intermediate representations (Python scripts, JSON) rather than direct 3D/2D output; multimodal inputs increasingly common (text+image most frequent). — [Zhang et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.08137)
- Trend: GPT-4o (16) and GPT-4 (14) most frequently used models in reviewed works. — [Zhang et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.08137)
- Challenge: datasets lack complexity/annotations; geometric errors; proprietary formats; only sketch+extrude ops supported vs. fillet/chamfer/loft/sweep. — [ICED25 challenges paper](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/proceedings-of-the-design-society/article/challenges-and-opportunities-in-the-integration-of-generative-ai-with-computeraided-design/7BFFBB14BB4DBB258A56CD03F3E3F7F5)
- Challenge: understanding design intent/context, intricate geometries, diverse datasets/benchmarks, interoperability, scalability, security, industrial applications. — [Zhou & Camba](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S095741742501142X)
- Challenge: geometric hallucination, topological validity, dataset scarcity, interpretability; need trustworthy human-in-the-loop. — [Assembly/MFR review](https://doi.org/10.52783/cana.v32.7270)
- Challenge: geometric validity & executability strong; parametric editability, design-intent preservation, feature-history robustness, downstream verification weakly benchmarked. — [Machines critical review 2026](https://doi.org/10.3390/machines14101206)
- Challenge: no agreed benchmark for text-to-CAD; Chamfer Distance misses editability/intent/manufacturability. — [cadarena eval review](https://cadarena.dev/eval-review.html); corroborated by [Zhang et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.08137) and [Heidari & Iosifidis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.17695)
- Quality maturity: most LLM-CAD approaches at maturity level 3 (parametric CAD scripts); none reach feature-based model history or assembly generation (6-level model, VDI 2209). — [DFX 2026 overview](https://doi.org/10.35199/dfx2026.8)
- Makatura et al. challenges: delicate balance of creativity vs formal verification; need DSL crafting, external APIs; iterative solutions. — [MIT](https://doi.org/10.21428/e4baedd9.745b62fa)
- Intelligent CAD 2.0: ICAD should be intensional (deeply engage designer intent) not just extensional; early-stage design support. — [Zou et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.03759)

### Inferences
- Evaluation gap is the central open challenge: geometric metrics dominate; engineering-grade criteria (editability, feature history, manufacturability) under-measured. Strong consensus across surveys.

### Gaps
- Limited quantitative cross-survey comparison of challenge severity; most are qualitative.

## Key Question 4: Taxonomies or frameworks proposed?

### Takeaway
Several taxonomies/frameworks: Zhang et al. 6-area application taxonomy + evaluation study; Wu et al. representation/condition-based taxonomy; EG-CAD 7-criteria framework; 6-level maturity model (DFX); ICAD 2.0 intensional/extensional framework; ICED25 8 use cases + challenge taxonomy; Klein et al. design-theory positioning; Mustapha's LLM-application taxonomy for mechanics/design/manufacturing.

### Cited Findings
- Zhang et al.: taxonomy of 6 key application areas (Data Generation, CAD Code Generation, Parametric CAD Generation, Image Generation, Model Evaluation, Text Generation); also analyzes data modalities, model usage trends, dataset sources, industrial domains. — [arXiv 2505.08137](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.08137)
- Wu et al.: organizes by representation (point cloud/mesh/surface/parametric) and condition (text/image/sketch/point cloud), ML→LLM. — [SciOpen](https://www.sciopen.com/article/10.26599/CVM.2025.9450521)
- EG-CAD framework (Machines 2026): geometric validity & executability as mandatory gates, then parametric editability, constraint/design-intent preservation, feature-history robustness, engineering compliance, downstream usability; plus reference agent architecture & staged roadmap. — [DOI 10.3390/machines14101206](https://doi.org/10.3390/machines14101206)
- 6-level maturity model (DFX 2026): grounded in VDI 2209; most approaches at level 3 (parametric CAD scripts). — [DOI 10.35199/dfx2026.8](https://doi.org/10.35199/dfx2026.8)
- ICAD 2.0 framework (Zou et al.): intensional vs extensional role of ICAD in design process; conceptual framework with components & challenges. — [arXiv 2410.03759](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.03759)
- ICED25 paper: 8 distinct GenAI-for-CAD use cases (CAD geometry generation, B-REP→parametric reverse engineering, generating variants, etc.) + challenge taxonomy. — [Cambridge Core](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/proceedings-of-the-design-society/article/challenges-and-opportunities-in-the-integration-of-generative-ai-with-computeraided-design/7BFFBB14BB4DBB258A56CD03F3E3F7F5)
- Klein et al.: positions generative AI for CAD within design process, designer intelligence theories, human cognition. — [IEEE CSDL](https://www.computer.org/csdl/magazine/cg/2026/03/11397452/2e9R0ceuB68)
- Heidari & Iosifidis: categories — similarity analysis & retrieval, 2D/3D synthesis, CAD from point clouds, single/multi-view images; plus dataset & code catalogs. — [arXiv 2402.17695](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.17695)
- Zhou & Camba: challenges taxonomy (design intent/context, intricate geometries, datasets/benchmarks, interoperability, scalability, security, industrial apps). — [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S095741742501142X)

### Inferences
- Convergence toward engineering-grade/editability criteria (EG-CAD, maturity model, cadarena eval review) as the field matures beyond geometric similarity.

### Gaps
- No single unified taxonomy accepted; surveys structure by application area vs. representation vs. design process. Harmonization open.
