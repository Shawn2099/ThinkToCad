### Gaps

- "CADAssembly" as a named benchmark could not be located on arXiv; may refer to a dataset name inside another paper — flagged unverified.

## Methods: multi-agent, construction graphs, connector-first, assembly GNNs

### Takeaway
Four method families dominate: (a) multi-agent pipelines with specialized Design/Generation/Assembly/Review agents; (b) intermediate structured representations (constraint intent trees, hierarchical geometry-aware graphs, assembly graphs); (c) connector/port/mate-first designs that fix joints before geometry; (d) graph neural networks over assembly/interface graphs.

### Cited Findings
- Multi-agent: ArtiCAD uses four agents (Design, Generation, Assembly, Review) plus a cross-stage rollback mechanism and a self-evolving experience store — [arXiv:2604.10992](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10992)
- Multi-agent: DFM-Redesign uses a DFM Reviewer + CAD Modifier loop with compile+visual verification per edit — [arXiv:2609.05559](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05559)
- Multi-agent: LMBuild agents retrieve/create/place components in an interactive environment — [arXiv:2610.04292](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04292)
- Multi-agent: ComActor self-correcting agent on ComCADBench — [arXiv:2606.13239](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13239)
- Intermediate representation (constraint intent tree): CIT-CAD infers a Constraint Intent Tree (entities, hierarchy, operations, relations) to guide code gen and verify — [arXiv:2609.07434](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07434)
- Intermediate representation (hierarchical geometry-aware graph): text→structure/constraints→action sequencing→code; 12K dataset — [arXiv:2604.10075](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10075)
- Engineering as Code: ADL with three sub-languages PDL (Part Definition), PML (Part Mating), PLL (Part Layout) — [arXiv:2609.04216](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04216)
- Assembly GNN (interface-augmented): Linkify builds assembly graphs with part nodes + interface-geometry edges; GATv2 for masked part prediction — [arXiv:2607.01205](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01205)
- Assembly GNN (material prediction): node-level material prediction over assembly graph on Fusion 360 Gallery — [arXiv:2209.12793](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.12793)
- Assembly GNN (function classification): assembly-flow graphs + GNN for component function — [arXiv:2107.07042](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.07042)
- Connector/port/mate-first: AssemCAD uses typed parts, geometry-backed ports, executable mates, engineering axioms + port/mate-based library with B-Rep evidence — [arXiv:2607.05123](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05123)
- Connector-first: ArtiCAD's Connector explicitly defines attachment points and joint parameters before geometry generation — [arXiv:2604.10992](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10992)

### Inferences
- "Connector-first" (ArtiCAD) and "port/mate-first" (AssemCAD) converge on the same idea: fix interface/joint semantics before synthesizing geometry, compensating for weak spatial reasoning in LLMs/VLMs.
- Construction-graph intermediates (CIT, hierarchical graphs) are the assembly analogue of sketch-extrude operation trees for parts.

### Gaps
- Whether connector-first consistently outperforms geometry-first on interference-free assembly is not quantified in abstracts; full papers needed.

## Benchmarks: MUSE, ComCADBench, CADAssembly, and others

### Takeaway
A wave of 2026 benchmarks targets assembly-level CAD quality: MUSE (manufacturability/functional/assemblability of B-Rep assemblies), ComCADBench (agents in real industrial CAD software via COM), plus CADWorld, CADEngBench, RealCADBench, ArtiCAD-Bench/CADPrompt/ACD, AssemBench, LMBuild.

### Cited Findings
- MUSE: complex editable B-Rep assemblies; three-stage protocol (code check → geometric check → design-intent alignment with rubrics); VLM judge validated by humans; project site https://dong7313.github.io/muse-benchmark/ — [arXiv:2605.28579](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28579)
- ComCADBench: first benchmark for agents operating real industrial CAD software; COM-as-Action paradigm; frontier models near-zero under GUI — [arXiv:2606.13239](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.13239)
- CADWorld: 200 tasks across 11 mechanical-CAD workflow categories in FreeCAD (incl. assembly, CAM, FEM); success via executable checks; best agent 17.5% vs 87.0% expert — [arXiv:2609.16251](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16251)
- CADEngBench: 300 parametric parts (600 tasks) + 150 body pairs; joint retrieval, face/edge grounding, joint-frame prediction, kinematic verification; FEA in CalculiX — [arXiv:2608.09296](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09296)
- RealCADBench: 12,632 tasks from 19 factory-automation categories; text/2D drawings/photos/renders; Part + Assembly (RCB-Assm25, 25-task assembly study) — [arXiv:2609.03773](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03773)
- ArtiCAD-Bench, CADPrompt, ACD: three datasets used to evaluate ArtiCAD — [arXiv:2604.10992](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10992)
- AssemBench: benchmark used by AssemCAD (size/metrics not in abstract) — [arXiv:2607.05123](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05123)
- LMBuild: benchmark for LLM agents generating buildable/functional assembled structures with joints/materials/sequences — [arXiv:2610.04292](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04292)

### Inferences
- Evaluation is shifting from geometric IoU (DeepCAD-style) toward executability + physical validity + design-intent rubrics (MUSE, CADEngBench).

### Gaps
- "CADAssembly" as a standalone named benchmark was not found on arXiv; may be an internal dataset name — flagged unverified. (Possibly confused with Fusion 360 Gallery Assembly dataset used by Linkify/material-prediction papers.)

## "Connector-first" assembly (ArtiCAD)

### Takeaway
ArtiCAD's key idea: predict assembly relationships in the design stage via a "Connector" that explicitly defines attachment points and joint parameters, before any geometry is generated — sidestepping LLM/VLM spatial-reasoning limits.

### Cited Findings
- ArtiCAD is the first training-free multi-agent system generating editable, articulated CAD assemblies from text/images; four agents: Design, Generation, Assembly, Review — [arXiv:2604.10992](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10992)
- Connector explicitly defines attachment points and joint parameters; determines relationships before geometry generation — [arXiv:2604.10992](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10992)
- Cross-stage rollback mechanism isolates and corrects design- and code-level errors; self-evolving experience store accumulates design knowledge — [arXiv:2604.10992](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10992)
- Supports URDF export for embodied-AI training assets — [arXiv:2604.10992](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10992)

### Inferences
- Connector-first is a design-time joint declaration: joints are parameters in the spec, not inferred post-hoc from geometry.

### Gaps
- Exact Connector schema/parameterization and supported joint types (revolute/prismatic/fixed/etc.) not detailed in abstract; full paper needed.

## Joints, mates, and interference handling

### Takeaway
Joints/mates are handled via explicit symbolic declarations (ports, mates, connectors, axioms) validated against B-Rep geometry; interference/physical validity is checked via geometric evidence, FEA, or kinematic verification.

### Cited Findings
- AssemCAD: geometry-backed ports + executable mates + engineering axioms; deterministic mate transformations; validates declared interfaces using concrete B-Rep geometric evidence — [arXiv:2607.05123](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05123)
- ArtiCAD: Connector defines joint parameters (revolute-style articulation) before geometry; URDF export implies kinematic joints — [arXiv:2604.10992](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10992)
- CADEngBench: assembly track evaluates ranked joint retrieval, exact face-and-edge grounding, joint-frame prediction, kinematic verification — [arXiv:2608.09296](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09296)
- Nova3D: articulates 59 joints across 12 assets at 98.3% geometric validity — [arXiv:2607.22738](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22738)
- Part-level vehicle generation predicts joint positions and hinge axes of movable parts — [arXiv:2604.05070](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.05070)
- Self-improving CAD agents validate assembled multi-part STEP via FEA — [arXiv:2605.17448](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.17448)
- Linkify recomputes high-fidelity interface geometry (contacts) for Fusion 360 Gallery Assembly dataset — [arXiv:2607.01205](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01205)

### Inferences
- Common pattern: declare joints symbolically (mate/connector/port) then verify against B-Rep/physics rather than inferring from raw geometry.

### Gaps
- Interference (collision) detection specifics (clearance thresholds, penetration checks) not described in abstracts; full papers needed.

## Assembly sequence planning with AI

### Takeaway
AI-driven ASP appears in two forms: (1) learning-based relation extraction + geometric-symbolic planning from CAD point clouds (Harlacher & Friedrich); (2) physics-simulator disassembly with DfA cost functions + MLLM manuals (von Arx et al.); plus multi-robot construction planning (Brown et al.).

### Cited Findings
- CAD-Based Relation Learning and Geometric-Symbolic Planning for Robotic Assembly: neural network predicts semantic geometric relations from point clouds; transformed into symbolic assembly graph; visibility-based ray-casting for disassembly directions; 85.83% planning success on ASAP test set; >50x speedup for >30 components — [arXiv:2609.17263](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17263)
- End-to-End Autonomous Generation of Human Assembly Plans: mesh assembly → tool list, sequence/subassemblies, manual, design feedback; physics-simulator disassembly with DfA cost function; 35% reduction in simulated assembly time on 136 assemblies of 5–30 parts; correct tool 88.6% — [arXiv:2610.09781](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09781)
- Large-Scale Multi-Robot Assembly Planning for Autonomous Manufacturing: CAD-like product spec → radial layout optimization, graph-repair MIP task allocation, collaborative carrying, distributed collision-free control; 1845-part Saturn V with 250 robots in <3 min — [arXiv:2311.18661](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.18661)
- Automated Assembly Instruction Generation from CAD Models Using Grounded LLMs: STEP assembly → typed ProductGraph → topological sort precedence → LLM step realization; human-in-the-loop (withdrawal requested) — [arXiv:2610.11896](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11896)

### Inferences
- ASP is treated as a separate (downstream) problem from assembly generation; few papers close the loop from generated assembly → sequence.

### Gaps
- No standardized ASP benchmark for CAD assemblies (ASAP test set is robotic disassembly); gap in AI-native assembly-sequence evaluation.
