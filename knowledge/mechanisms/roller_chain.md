# roller_chain

**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** Provide positive (no-slip) power transmission via metallic chain wrapping sprockets.

## How it works
Metallic links (rollers on pins) wrap around sprockets; positive drive, no slip. Requires lubrication and adequate wrap; chordal action causes speed fluctuation.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Minimum wrap angle >= 120deg on small sprocket.
- Sprocket minimum tooth counts 12-25 by speed to mitigate chordal action.
- Recommended center distance ~30-50x chain pitch; generally not exceed ~80x pitch without tensioner.
- Chain tension: 2-3% mid-span movement of center distance (horizontal).
- Efficiency ~98% at proper speed.

## Constraints
- Requires lubrication; dirty environments accelerate wear.
- Chordal action -> speed fluctuation; mitigate with more teeth.
- Fixed center distance needs tensioner or adjustable mount.

## Failure modes
- Wear elongation causing slack and skipping.
- Chordal vibration and noise.
- Sprocket tooth wear; lubrication failure.

## When to use
High-torque/dirty environments: conveyors, bicycles, agricultural machinery.

## DFM (small workshop)
Size sprockets >=12-17 teeth, keep wrap >=120deg, include tension adjustment, lubricate.
