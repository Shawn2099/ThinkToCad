# watt_linkage
**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** Approximate straight-line motion via three-bar parallel motion.

## How it works
Equal side links with a coupler; midpoint traces a near-straight arc.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Equal side links.
- Coupler length.

## Constraints
- Approximate; limited stroke.

## Failure modes
- Deviation from straight line.
- Joint wear.

## When to use
Steam engine piston guidance; suspensions.

## DFM (small workshop)
Ensure equal link lengths; rigid pivots.
