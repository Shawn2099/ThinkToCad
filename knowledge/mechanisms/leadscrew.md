# leadscrew
**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** Convert rotary to linear motion via screw and nut thread.

## How it works
Rotating screw drives nut along axis; thread form (Acme/trapezoidal) sets efficiency and load.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Pitch, lead, thread angle, nut length.
- Efficiency ~30-50%.

## Constraints
- Low efficiency.
- Backdriving possible; wear.

## Failure modes
- Thread wear.
- Backlash; galling.

## When to use
Vises, jacks, CNC axes, low speed high load.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use standard Acme rod/nut; lubricate; support ends.
