# hypoid_gear
**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** Skew-axis bevel gear with offset pinion for high strength and quietness.

## How it works
Hyperbolic pitch surface; pinion offset below/above crown wheel; sliding action.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Offset; ratio; spiral angle.

## Constraints
- Needs EP oil.
- Precise setup; efficiency trade-off.

## Failure modes
- Wear if poor lubrication.
- Overheating.

## When to use
Automotive final drives, railcar transmissions.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use EP oil; precise shimming.
