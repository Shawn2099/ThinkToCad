# snap_action

**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** Bistable over-center spring/lever mechanism that snaps rapidly between two states for switches, valves, and thermostats.

## How it works
Over-center spring and lever device; main and secondary levers fulcrumed and connected by spring, with optional magnet restraint of the secondary lever until sufficient spring tension. Rapidly toggles between stable states.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Spring stiffness; lever geometry; magnet restraint (patent US2405014A).

## Constraints
- Limited stroke; friction and wear at pivots.

## Failure modes
- Spring fatigue; pivot wear; failure to snap if spring weakened.

## When to use
- Switches, valves, thermostats, rapid switching applications.

## DFM (small workshop)
- Use standard torsion/extension spring and simple lever; ensure over-center geometry.
