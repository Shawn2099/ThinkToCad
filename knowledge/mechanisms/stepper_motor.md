# stepper_motor

**Type:** actuator
**Function:** Electricity -> discrete rotary.

## How it works
Electricity -> discrete rotary. Step angle, holding torque, speed

## Key dimensions / equations
- Step angle, holding torque, speed

## Constraints
- Torque drops with speed
- resonance
- stall

## Failure modes
- Torque drops with speed; resonance; stall

## When to use
- Applications: Printers, CNC, robotics, indexing - [Firgelli](https://www.firgelli.com/pages/actuators); [Tolomatic](https://www.tolomatic.com/info-center/resource-details/choosing-stepper-servo-actuators-to-replace-air-cylinders)
- Advantages: Open-loop precision; no feedback needed

## DFM (small workshop)
Use standard parts and simple tooling; verify fit and tolerances in the small workshop.
