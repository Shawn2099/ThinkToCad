# deep_groove_ball

**Type:** bearing
**Function:** Support combined radial and moderate axial loads with low friction in a compact, low-cost package.

## How it works
Deep-groove (Conrad) ball bearing: balls roll between inner and outer rings with deep raceway grooves, allowing radial load plus moderate axial load in both directions. Sealed or shielded; grease lubricated.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Basic rating life L10 = (C/P)^3 million revolutions (ball bearings, p = 3).
- C = basic dynamic load rating; P = equivalent dynamic load.
- Fits: shaft g6/h6/j6/k5/m5/n6; housing H7/J7/K7.
- Rotating inner ring -> tighter shaft fit (k5/m5/n6); rotating outer ring -> tighter housing fit.

## Constraints
- Axial load capacity limited vs. angular contact.
- Speed and temperature limits by seal/lubricant.
- Requires proper fit to avoid creep/slip.

## Failure modes
- Fatigue pitting (L10 life exceeded).
- Brinelling from shock/overload.
- Seal failure and lubricant loss.

## When to use
General-purpose shafts O10-40 mm; motors, gearboxes, pumps, workshop machines.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use 60xx/62xx series off-the-shelf; rubber seals for dusty/wet, shields for high speed; grease (NLGI 2).
