# leaf_spring

**Type:** spring
**Function:** Provide suspension/return force using flat spring steel leaves in bending.

## How it works
One or more flat steel leaves clamped at ends; load bends leaves, storing energy. Common in vehicle suspensions and simple returns. Can be single or multi-leaf (laminated).

## Key dimensions / equations
- Bending stress sigma = M*c/I; deflection depends on length, thickness, modulus.
- Multi-leaf improves capacity and damping (interleaf friction).
- Stress concentration at clamps and ends.

## Constraints
- Requires space; bulky vs. coil springs.
- Interleaf friction causes hysteresis.
- Corrosion protection needed.

## Failure modes
- Fatigue fracture at clamps/ends.
- Settling and sag over time.
- Corrosion pitting leading to fatigue.

## When to use
Vehicle suspensions, simple returns, clamps, heavy-duty applications.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use standard spring steel, avoid sharp corners, clamp securely, protect from corrosion.
