# constant_force_spring

**Type:** spring
**Function:** Exert nearly constant force over a long extension by unrolling a tightly coiled ribbon.

## How it works
A thin ribbon is wound tightly on a drum; as it unrolls, the strip bends and the resisting torque at the drum yields an approximately constant pulling force over most of the stroke.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Force F roughly constant (manufacturer-rated) over working extension.
- Stroke limited by strip length and drum diameter.
- Torque at drum = F * drum radius.

## Constraints
- Force varies slightly with extension; not perfectly constant.
- Requires drum and guide to prevent sideways drift.
- Strip fatigue over many cycles.

## Failure modes
- Strip fatigue or kinking.
- Inconsistent force from improper winding.
- Drum wear or jamming.

## When to use
Counterbalances, retractable tape measures, window sashes, typewriter carriage returns.

## DFM (small workshop)
Buy rated spring assemblies; provide straight guide and adequate drum support; avoid sharp bends.
