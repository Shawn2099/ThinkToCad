# air_bearing

**Type:** bearing
**Function:** Gas (air) film separates surfaces; non-contact; very low friction - [Wikipedia, Bearing (mechanical)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bearing_(mechanical)) (fluid bearing).

## How it works
Gas (air) film separates surfaces; non-contact; very low friction - [Wikipedia, Bearing (mechanical)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bearing_(mechanical)) (fluid bearing).

## Key dimensions / equations
- Low-to-moderate; high stiffness.

## Constraints
- Low load
- contamination sensitivity
- air supply.

## Failure modes
- Low load, contamination sensitivity, air supply.

## When to use
- Applications: CMMs, precision spindles, lithography.
- Select when: Ultra-precision, clean, light load.
- Advantages: Zero friction, ultra-precision, clean.
