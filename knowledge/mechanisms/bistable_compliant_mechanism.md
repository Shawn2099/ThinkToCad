# bistable_compliant_mechanism

**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** 1 DOF (two stable states).

## How it works
1 DOF (two stable states). Flexure geometry; strain energy

## Key dimensions / equations
- Flexure geometry; strain energy

## Constraints
- Limited stroke/rotation (flexures ~10-20deg)

## Failure modes
- Limited stroke/rotation (flexures ~10-20deg)

## When to use
- Applications: Switches, closures, hinges, grippers - [BYU](https://www.et.byu.edu/~bdjensen/publications/byubistablepaper.pdf); [compliantmechanisms.byu.edu](https://compliantmechanisms.byu.edu/about-compliant-mechanisms)
- Advantages: Zero-power holding; low part count; no backlash

## DFM (small workshop)
Use standard parts and simple tooling; verify fit and tolerances in the small workshop.
