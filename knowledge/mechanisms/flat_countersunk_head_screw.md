# flat_countersunk_head_screw

**Type:** fastener
**Function:** Conical head seats flush in countersunk hole; Phillips/slotted/hex drive; used where protrusion is unacceptable - [Fastener Technical Manual (DIN 965/ISO 7046)](https://classccomponents.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Fastener-Technical-Manual.pdf); [UF fasteners lab notes](https://web.mae.ufl.edu/designlab/lab%20assignments/eml2322l-fasteners.pdf).

## How it works
Conical head seats flush in countersunk hole; Phillips/slotted/hex drive; used where protrusion is unacceptable - [Fastener Technical Manual (DIN 965/ISO 7046)](https://classccomponents.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Fastener-Technical-Manual.pdf); [UF fasteners lab notes](https://web.mae.ufl.edu/designlab/lab%20assignments/eml2322l-fasteners.pdf).

## Key dimensions / equations
- Moderate; head bearing distributes load in thin sheet; not for heavy tension.

## Constraints
- Weaker head
- harder to drive
- countersink required.

## Failure modes
- Weaker head, harder to drive, countersink required.

## When to use
- Applications: Sheet metal, aircraft skins, consumer products.
- Select when: Flush surface required, light-to-moderate loads.
- Advantages: Flush surface, good appearance, aerodynamic.

Use standard parts and simple tooling; verify fit and tolerances in the small workshop.
