# seam_weld

**Type:** joint
**Function:** Continuous or overlapping spot welds along seam; can be watertight - [Fronius blog](https://blog.fronius.com/welding/en/know-how/types-of-welding-joints-and-seams/) (seam types).

## How it works
Continuous or overlapping spot welds along seam; can be watertight - [Fronius blog](https://blog.fronius.com/welding/en/know-how/types-of-welding-joints-and-seams/) (seam types).

## Key dimensions / equations
- Moderate; leak-tight seam.

## Constraints
- Distortion
- slower than spot
- sheet thickness limits.

## Failure modes
- Distortion, slower than spot, sheet thickness limits.

## When to use
- Applications: Tanks, pipes, containers.
- Select when: Leak-tight sheet joints.
- Advantages: Sealed joints, continuous strength.
