# ball_screw
**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** Convert rotary to linear motion with recirculating balls for high efficiency and precision.

## How it works
Balls roll between screw and nut and recirculate; low friction.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Ball diameter, lead, preload, accuracy grade.

## Constraints
- Cost.
- Contamination sensitivity; backdriving.

## Failure modes
- Ball/track wear.
- Contamination; brinelling.

## When to use
CNC, robotics, precision positioning.

## DFM (small workshop)
Seal ends; keep clean; preload carefully.
