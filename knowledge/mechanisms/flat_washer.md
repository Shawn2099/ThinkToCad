# flat_washer

**Type:** fastener
**Function:** Thin annular disc to distribute bolt load, protect surface, provide bearing surface; standards ISO 7089/7090, DIN 125 - [Fastener Technical Manual](https://classccomponents.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Fastener-Technical-Manual.pdf); [ANSI washer standards](https://webstore.ansi.org/industry/fasteners/washers).

## How it works
Thin annular disc to distribute bolt load, protect surface, provide bearing surface; standards ISO 7089/7090, DIN 125 - [Fastener Technical Manual](https://classccomponents.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Fastener-Technical-Manual.pdf); [ANSI washer standards](https://webstore.ansi.org/industry/fasteners/washers).

## Key dimensions / equations
- Increases bearing area; reduces surface crushing.

## Constraints
- Can relax
- not a lock by itself.

## Failure modes
- Can relax; not a lock by itself.

## When to use
- Applications: All bolted joints.
- Select when: Soft materials, large holes, surface protection.
- Advantages: Protects parts, improves load distribution, cheap.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use standard parts and simple tooling; verify fit and tolerances in the small workshop.
