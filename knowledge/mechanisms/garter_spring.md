# garter_spring

**Type:** spring
**Function:** Provide radial sealing/retention force in a circular form (helical extension spring closed into a ring).

## How it works
A helical spring formed into a circle; ends joined. It exerts inward/outward radial force when stretched around a shaft/bore or seal.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Radial force scales with spring rate and installed stretch.
- Diameter change produces force (F ~ k x).
- Fatigue depends on cyclic diameter change.

## Constraints
- Requires proper installation diameter.
- Limited to radial sealing/retention.
- End joint must be secure.

## Failure modes
- End joint failure.
- Fatigue from cyclic stretch.
- Corrosion in seal environments.

## When to use
Seals, retaining rings, shaft/bore retention.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use commercial garter springs; size to installed diameter; avoid over-stretch.
