# constant_lead_barrel_cam
**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** Produce follower position linear with rotation (like a lead screw) in a cylindrical cam.

## How it works
Groove cut in cylinder with constant lead; follower rides groove.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Lead; cylinder diameter; groove width.

## Constraints
- Groove machining.
- Friction; wear.

## Failure modes
- Groove wear.
- Follower binding.

## When to use
Lathe threading cross-slide settings.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use hardened cylinder; lubricate.
