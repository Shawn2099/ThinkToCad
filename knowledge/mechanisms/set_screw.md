## How it works
Headless screw threaded into part to clamp/lock another part (shaft in hub); point styles flat/cone/cup/dog - [Fastener Technical Manual (DIN 913-916/ISO 4026-4029)](https://classccomponents.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Fastener-Technical-Manual.pdf).

## Key dimensions / equations
- Clamping friction; not for high torque alone.

## Constraints
- Can damage shaft
- loosens under vibration
- low torque capacity alone.

## Failure modes
- Can damage shaft, loosens under vibration, low torque capacity alone.

## When to use
- Applications: Collars, gears on shafts, adjustable components.
- Select when: Positioning/locking, light-to-moderate torque (with key for high torque).
- Advantages: Compact, flush, adjustable.

Use standard parts and simple tooling; verify fit and tolerances in the small workshop.
