# quick_release

**Type:** indexing
**Function:** Convert small lever motion into large clamping force via an eccentric cam (quick-release skewer).

## How it works
An eccentric cam lever produces large clamping force from small handle motion; used on bicycle wheels and clamps. Release by flipping lever, freeing the cam.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Clamping force depends on cam eccentricity and lever angle (empirical).
- Small handle motion -> large normal force via eccentricity.
- Release instant when lever passes over-center.

## Constraints
- Clamping force vs. lever angle not standardized; test empirically.
- Wear at cam/pivot.
- Must be fully closed to lock.

## Failure modes
- Partial closure -> insufficient clamping.
- Cam/pivot wear reducing force.
- Accidental opening under vibration.

## When to use
Bicycle wheels, quick clamps, removable fixtures, tool-free assembly.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use hardened cam, provide full-closure indication, test clamping force.
