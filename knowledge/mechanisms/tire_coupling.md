# tire_coupling

**Type:** coupling
**Function:** Transmit torque through a highly flexible elastomeric tire element between hubs.

## How it works
An elastomeric tire element sits between hubs, allowing large angular/parallel/axial misalignment and damping with no metal contact.

## Key dimensions / equations
- High misalignment capacity (angular/parallel/axial).
- Torque moderate; speed limited by elastomer.
- Damping from elastomer hysteresis.

## Constraints
- Elastomer aging/temperature limits.
- Lower torque/speed than metallic.
- No lubrication needed.

## Failure modes
- Tire cracking/aging.
- Overload tearing.
- Chemical/heat degradation.

## When to use
Compressors, pumps, conveyors, marine.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use commercial tires; protect from heat/chemicals; inspect periodically.
