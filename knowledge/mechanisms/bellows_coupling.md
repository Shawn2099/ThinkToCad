# bellows_coupling

**Type:** coupling
**Function:** Transmit torque through a thin-walled metal bellows with zero backlash and low inertia.

## How it works
Two hubs joined by a stainless-steel bellows; bellows flexes to accommodate angular, parallel, and axial misalignment while transmitting torque.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Align within ~0.2-0.4 mm parallel for infinite fatigue life.
- Axial travel up to ~0.4 mm typical.
- Torque limited by bellows wall thickness.

## Constraints
- Low damping; not for damping needs.
- Sensitive to parallel misalignment.
- Higher cost.

## Failure modes
- Bellows fatigue from misalignment.
- Buckling if over-deflected.
- Seal/contamination issues.

## When to use
Servo/encoder, semiconductor, medical, high-speed precision.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use commercial units; align carefully; avoid shock loads.
