# finger_spring

**Type:** spring
**Function:** Provide preload or retention using flat spring fingers (cantilever) in a washer form.

## How it works
Thin fingers cut/formed in a flat washer act as cantilever springs; when compressed they exert axial preload/retention force on adjacent parts.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Force per finger ~ E b t^3 delta / (4 L^3) (cantilever approx).
- Preload set by compression deflection.
- Number of fingers scales load.

## Constraints
- Limited travel; over-compression causes set.
- Finger fatigue under cyclic load.
- Requires flat mating surfaces.

## Failure modes
- Finger fatigue or fracture.
- Loss of preload from set.
- Corrosion in thin sections.

## When to use
Bearing preload, fastener security, small axial retention.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use commercial wave/finger washers; ensure correct orientation and flat seats.
