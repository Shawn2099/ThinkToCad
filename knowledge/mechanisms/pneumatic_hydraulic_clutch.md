# pneumatic_hydraulic_clutch

**Type:** clutch
**Function:** Engage a multi-plate or cone clutch remotely using air or oil pressure.

## How it works
Pneumatic/hydraulic pressure actuates a piston that clamps friction plates or a cone. Allows fast, remote engagement in industrial automation.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Clamping force F = p * A_piston.
- Torque T = mu F r_m n (plates).
- Engagement time set by flow rate and volume.

## Constraints
- Requires pressure source and seals.
- Heat dissipation for slipping.
- Air compressibility affects response.

## Failure modes
- Seal leakage.
- Contamination in hydraulic fluid/air.
- Plate wear from slip.

## When to use
Industrial automation, remote engagement, high-duty drives.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use commercial actuated clutches; filter air/oil; size compressor/pump accordingly.
