# snail_drop_cam
**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** Raise follower over 24 h in spiral path then sharp drop to advance day.

## How it works
Snail cam raises follower slowly; solid follower causes precise drop at midnight.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Spiral path; drop height; timing angle.

## Constraints
- Timing precision; wear.

## Failure modes
- Mistimed drop.
- Wear at drop edge.

## When to use
Clocking-in clocks, day advance.

## DFM (small workshop)
Harden drop edge; adjust timing.
