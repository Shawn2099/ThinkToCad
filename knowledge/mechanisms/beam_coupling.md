# beam_coupling

**Type:** coupling
**Function:** Provide backlash-free, flexible power transmission for precision motion control.

## How it works
Single-piece spiral/helical beam cut into a cylinder; beam flexes to accommodate angular, parallel, and axial misalignment while transmitting torque with zero backlash.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Accommodates angular/parallel/axial misalignment in one piece.
- Materials: Al, stainless steel, acetal, titanium.
- Torque capacity depends on beam geometry and material.

## Constraints
- Limited torque capacity vs. jaw/gear couplings.
- Beam fatigue under cyclic/reversing loads.
- Not for high shock loads.

## When to use
Encoders, CNC axes, precision motion, small motors.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use off-the-shelf beam couplings; match bore to shaft; avoid over-torque and shock.
