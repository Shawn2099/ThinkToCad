## How it works
Domed head with slotted/Phillips drive; mates with tapped hole or nut; common in small assemblies - [Fastener Technical Manual](https://classccomponents.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Fastener-Technical-Manual.pdf).

## Key dimensions / equations
- Low-to-moderate tension/shear.

## Constraints
- Low torque capacity
- head protrudes.

## Failure modes
- Low torque capacity, head protrudes.

## When to use
- Applications: Electronics, panels, light machinery.
- Select when: Light loads, general assembly, low cost.
- Advantages: Inexpensive, simple, no special tools.
