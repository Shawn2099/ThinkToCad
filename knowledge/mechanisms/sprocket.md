# sprocket

**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** Engage and drive a roller chain (or belt) to transmit power, with tooth count setting ratio and chordal behavior.

## How it works
A wheel with teeth shaped to mesh with chain rollers. Tooth count determines speed ratio and chordal action severity; minimum tooth counts (12-25 by speed) mitigate speed fluctuation.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Speed ratio = tooth counts (chain) or pitch diameters.
- Wrap angle >= 120deg on small sprocket.
- Minimum teeth 12-25 by speed.
- Center distance ~30-50x pitch; <=80x pitch without tensioner.

## Constraints
- Small sprockets (<12 teeth) cause severe chordal action and wear.
- Alignment critical to avoid side wear.
- Material choice affects wear life.

## Failure modes
- Tooth wear and hooking.
- Chain skipping on worn teeth.
- Side wear from misalignment.

## When to use
Chain drives, indexing, conveyors, bicycles.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use off-the-shelf sprockets, keep teeth >=12-17, align shafts, lubricate chain.
