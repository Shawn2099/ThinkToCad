# diaphragm_coupling

**Type:** coupling
**Function:** Transmit torque through thin metal diaphragms/discs with zero backlash and high torsional stiffness.

## How it works
Torque passes across flexible metal diaphragms or disc packs clamped between hubs. Accommodates angular and axial misalignment; used at high speed.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Misalignment capacity angular/axial; limited parallel.
- High torsional stiffness; zero backlash.
- Speed ratings often >=3000 rpm for turbines.

## Constraints
- Low damping.
- Sensitive to misalignment.
- Expensive; precision alignment.

## Failure modes
- Diaphragm/disc fatigue.
- Bolt loosening.
- Misalignment-induced failure.

## When to use
Turbines, compressors, pumps, high-speed rotating machinery.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use commercial units; align within spec; inspect for cracks.
