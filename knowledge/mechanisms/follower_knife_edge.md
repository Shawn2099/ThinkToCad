# follower_knife_edge
**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** Follow cam profile with a sharp edge for high precision at low load.

## How it works
Knife-edge follower contacts cam with minimal contact area.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Contact stress; wear rate.

## Constraints
- High wear; low load.
- Lubrication critical.

## Failure modes
- Rapid wear.
- Chipping.

## When to use
Low-load precision cams.

## DFM (small workshop)
Harden follower; lubricate.

## Sources
- Standard classification (Norton/Shigley).
