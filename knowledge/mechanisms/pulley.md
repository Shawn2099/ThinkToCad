# pulley

**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** Transfer motion via belts and set speed/torque ratios.

## How it works
A wheel (flat, V-groove, timing, or multi-groove) carries a belt; ratio determined by pitch diameters. Taper bushings allow quick mount/dismount.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Speed ratio i = D_driven / D_driver.
- Torque ratio inverse of speed ratio.
- Belt tension and wrap angle set power capacity.

## Constraints
- Friction belts slip under overload.
- Wear and tension maintenance.
- Alignment critical.

## Failure modes
- Belt slip/wear.
- Bearing wear.
- Misalignment damage.

## When to use
HVAC, automotive, conveyors, general industry.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use off-the-shelf pulleys/bushings; align shafts; provide tension adjustment.
