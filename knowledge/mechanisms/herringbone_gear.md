# herringbone_gear
**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** Double-helical gear with no axial thrust.

## How it works
Two helical gears of opposite hand side-by-side (V teeth).

## Key dimensions / equations
- Helix angle; face width.

## Constraints
- Manufacturing cost.
- Central gap/Wuest apex.

## Failure modes
- Tooth wear.
- Alignment errors.

## When to use
Marine turbines, heavy power transmission.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use commercial; precise alignment.
