# crown_gear
**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** Gear with teeth at right angles to plane; meshes with escapements/clocks.

## How it works
Crown (contrate) gear teeth project perpendicular to disk; meshes with pinion.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Pitch diameter; tooth count.

## Constraints
- Low speed.
- Impact noise.

## Failure modes
- Tooth wear.
- Breakage.

## When to use
Clocks, escapements.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use brass for clocks; careful tooth forming.
