# spot_weld

**Type:** joint
**Function:** Resistance weld at discrete points joining sheet; used in automotive bodies - [KEVOS](https://kevos.com/insights/choosing-a-joining-method/) (hybrid weld-bonding); [Ideambox](https://ideambox.com/assets/downloads/welded-adhesive-joints.pdf).

## How it works
Resistance weld at discrete points joining sheet; used in automotive bodies - [KEVOS](https://kevos.com/insights/choosing-a-joining-method/) (hybrid weld-bonding); [Ideambox](https://ideambox.com/assets/downloads/welded-adhesive-joints.pdf).

## Key dimensions / equations
- Moderate shear per spot; many spots share load.

## Constraints
- Local stress concentration
- fatigue
- access for electrodes.

## Failure modes
- Local stress concentration, fatigue, access for electrodes.

## When to use
- Applications: Automotive body-in-white, sheet metal assemblies.
- Select when: High-volume thin sheet, automated production.
- Advantages: Fast, automatable, no consumables, thin sheet.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use standard parts and simple tooling; verify fit and tolerances in the small workshop.
