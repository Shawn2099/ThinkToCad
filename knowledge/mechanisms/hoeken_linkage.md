# hoeken_linkage
**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** Provide approximately constant horizontal velocity over part of the stroke.

## How it works
Four-bar variant with Hoeken proportions; coupler point has near-constant horizontal velocity.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Hoeken proportions.

## Constraints
- Approximate; limited range.

## Failure modes
- Speed variation.
- Joint wear.

## When to use
Conveyors, walking machines.
