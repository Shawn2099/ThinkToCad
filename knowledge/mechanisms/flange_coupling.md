# flange_coupling

**Type:** coupling
**Function:** Connect shafts via bolted flanges for very high torque transmission.

## How it works
Two flanges keyed to shafts are bolted together, forming a rigid connection. Unbolt for inspection/service.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Torque limited by bolt shear/tension and flange.
- Bolt circle diameter and bolt count size capacity.
- Alignment critical.

## Constraints
- Rigid (no misalignment).
- Heavy and bulky.
- Backlash possible at bolted interface.

## Failure modes
- Bolt failure.
- Flange cracking.
- Misalignment damage.

## When to use
Turbines, pumps, marine propulsion, generator sets.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use precision-machined flanges; torque bolts evenly; align carefully.
