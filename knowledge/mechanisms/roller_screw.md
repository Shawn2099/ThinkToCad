# roller_screw
**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** Convert rotary to linear motion with threaded rollers for extreme load and stiffness.

## How it works
Threaded rollers between screw and nut transmit load.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Roller count, lead, load rating.

## Constraints
- Cost; complexity.

## Failure modes
- Roller wear.
- Alignment errors.

## When to use
Aerospace actuators, heavy machinery.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use manufacturer assemblies; precise alignment.
