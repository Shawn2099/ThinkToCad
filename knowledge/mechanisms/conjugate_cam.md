# conjugate_cam
**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** Eliminate backlash using paired cams with two followers.

## How it works
Two cams/followers work together for positive positioning.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Cam pair geometry; follower phasing.

## Constraints
- Cost; complexity.

## Failure modes

- Wear.

## When to use
Precision motion.
