# differential
**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** Averaging gear allowing output speed to equal average of two inputs.

## How it works
Three-shaft gear train; open or limited-slip variants.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Gear ratio; torque bias.

## Constraints
- Complexity.
- Traction limits (open).

## Failure modes
- Gear wear.
- Limited-slip clutch wear.

## When to use
Vehicles, clocks, analog computers.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use standard assemblies; lubricate.
