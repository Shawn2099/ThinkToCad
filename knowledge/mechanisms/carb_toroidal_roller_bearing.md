# carb_toroidal_roller_bearing

**Type:** bearing
**Function:** Toroidal rollers; accommodates misalignment and axial displacement; non-locating - [Wikipedia, Rolling-element bearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling-element_bearing); SKF 1995.

## How it works
Toroidal rollers; accommodates misalignment and axial displacement; non-locating - [Wikipedia, Rolling-element bearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling-element_bearing); SKF 1995.

## Key dimensions / equations
- High radial; no axial location.

## Constraints
- Non-locating
- must pair with locating bearing.

## Failure modes
- Non-locating; must pair with locating bearing.

## When to use
- Applications: Long shafts, paper machines, thermal expansion.
- Select when: Non-locating bearing paired with locating.
- Advantages: Thermal expansion accommodation, misalignment.
