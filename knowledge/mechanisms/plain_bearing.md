# plain_bearing

**Type:** bearing
**Function:** Support a shaft by sliding contact, simple and low-cost, with lubrication forming a film.

## How it works
A sleeve/bushing (bronze, PTFE, or babbitt) supports the shaft; lubricant separates surfaces under hydrodynamic or boundary lubrication. No rolling elements; compact and tolerant of shock.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Design basis: PV (pressure x velocity) limits by material; hydrodynamic film thickness.
- Clearance fits per ISO 286 / manufacturer (e.g., H7/f7, H7/g6).
- L10 life not applicable; wear-based life.

## Constraints
- Requires continuous lubrication; dry running causes rapid wear.
- Surface speed and load limits (PV).
- Less precise than rolling bearings.

## Failure modes
- Wear and scoring from lubrication failure.
- Overheating/seizure.
- Contamination embedding in soft bearing.

## When to use
Low speed, high shock, dirty environments, simple/cheap builds, pivot bushings.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use bronze/PTFE bushings, oil holes/grooves, generous clearance, grease fittings; avoid over-speed.
