# spiral_bevel_gear
**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** Bevel gear with curved teeth for smoother, quieter operation.

## How it works
Helical/curved teeth on conical pitch surface; opposite hands for pinion/gear.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Spiral angle; module; cone angle.

## Constraints
- Replaced in pairs.
- Mounting precision.

## Failure modes
- Tooth wear/pitting.
- Noise if misaligned.

## When to use
Differentials, high-speed bevel.

## DFM (small workshop)
Buy matched sets; precise shimming.
