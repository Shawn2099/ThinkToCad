# solenoid

**Type:** actuator
**Function:** Electricity -> linear/rotary (on/off).

## How it works
Electricity -> linear/rotary (on/off). Coil voltage, stroke, force

## Key dimensions / equations
- Coil voltage, stroke, force

## Constraints
- Short stroke
- on/off only (proportional rare)

## Failure modes
- Short stroke; on/off only (proportional rare)

## When to use
- Applications: Valves, locks, relays, latches - [Firgelli](https://www.firgelli.com/pages/actuators)
- Advantages: Fast switching; simple; compact

## DFM (small workshop)
Use standard parts and simple tooling; verify fit and tolerances in the small workshop.
