# flexible_jaw_coupling

**Type:** coupling
**Function:** Transmit torque while accommodating misalignment and vibration via an elastomeric spider.

## How it works
Two jawed hubs with an elastomeric spider between them; spider absorbs misalignment, shock, and vibration. Common, cheap, moderate misalignment capacity (~1.5deg angular).

## Key dimensions / equations
- Accommodates up to ~1.5deg angular misalignment and some parallel offset.
- Torque capacity depends on spider material/hardness and jaw engagement.
- Backlash depends on spider fit.

## Constraints
- Spider wear/aging (elastomer).
- Limited misalignment capacity.
- Temperature limits of elastomer.

## Failure modes
- Spider cracking/tearing.
- Jaw wear from overload.
- Backlash increase as spider wears.

## When to use
General-purpose shafts <=25 mm, pumps, fans, conveyors.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use off-the-shelf jaw couplings; keep spare spiders; ensure alignment within capacity.
