# piezoelectric

**Type:** actuator
**Function:** Electricity -> micro displacement/force.

## How it works
Electricity -> micro displacement/force. Voltage, stroke (um), blocking force, stiffness

## Key dimensions / equations
- Voltage, stroke (um), blocking force, stiffness

## Constraints
- Small stroke
- high voltage
- brittle

## Failure modes
- Small stroke; high voltage; brittle

## When to use
- Applications: Nanopositioning, inkjet, optics, micro-robotics - [ejosat](https://doi.org/10.31590/ejosat.653751)
- Advantages: us response; high stiffness; no lubrication; DC

## DFM (small workshop)
Use standard parts and simple tooling; verify fit and tolerances in the small workshop.
