# spacer_sleeve

**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** Raise a deck/plate vertically without moving the motor or shaft.

## How it works
Stack of precision-ground sleeves/washers between the mounting flange and the frame.
Height change = sum of sleeve thicknesses.

## Constraints
- Requires flat, parallel mounting faces.
- Stack height must not exceed bolt grip length.
- Adds mass at the mount point.

## Failure modes
- Sleeve tilts if faces not parallel -> interference with shaft.
- Bolt too short -> incomplete thread engagement.

## When to use
Small height changes (< 20 mm), workshop-friendly, no machining needed.
