# split_muff_coupling

**Type:** coupling
**Function:** Clamp two shafts with a sleeve split into two bolted halves for easy assembly.

## How it works
Sleeve is split axially into two halves bolted around shafts, allowing installation without moving equipment. Rigid (no misalignment).

## Key dimensions / equations
- Torque lower than solid sleeve.
- Bolt torque sets clamping force.
- Alignment still critical.

## Constraints
- Rigid (no misalignment).
- Bolt maintenance.
- Lower torque than solid sleeve.

## Failure modes
- Bolt loosening.
- Fretting at interface.
- Misalignment damage.

## When to use
Heavy industrial where disassembly must be easy.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use high-grade bolts; torque evenly; align before tightening.
