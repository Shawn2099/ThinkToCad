**Type:** bearing
**Function:** Barrel rollers in spherical outer race; accommodates misalignment - [Wikipedia, Rolling-element bearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling-element_bearing).

## How it works
Barrel rollers in spherical outer race; accommodates misalignment - [Wikipedia, Rolling-element bearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling-element_bearing).

## Key dimensions / equations
- High radial + moderate axial; misalignment-tolerant.

## Constraints
- Higher friction/cost
- sliding.

## Failure modes
- Higher friction/cost, sliding.

## When to use
- Applications: Heavy industry, conveyors, wind turbines.
- Select when: Heavy load with misalignment.
- Advantages: Misalignment compensation, heavy load.
