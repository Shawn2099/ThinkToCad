# wrap_spring_clutch

**Type:** clutch
**Function:** Transmit torque one direction by tightening a helical spring onto a cylindrical sleeve; slips the other way.

## How it works
A helical (often square-wire) spring wraps tightly on a sleeve when driven one way (capstan effect) and loosens/slips when driven the other. Low cost, light-duty, low speed.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Torque to slip grows exponentially with turns (capstan: T_hold/T_slip = e^(mu theta)).
- Engagement by wrap; release by reverse torque.
- Spring preload sets initial grip.

## Constraints
- Light-duty, low speed only.
- Direction-specific; reverse freewheels/slips.
- Heat and wear if slipping.

## Failure modes
- Spring fatigue or permanent set.
- Sleeve wear/scoring.
- Slippage under overload.

## When to use
Low-cost one-way or single-revolution clutches, light machinery.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use commercial wrap-spring clutches; keep sleeve smooth and lubricated per vendor.
