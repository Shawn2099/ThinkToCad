# gear_coupling

**Type:** coupling
**Function:** Transmit very high torque via meshing internal/external gear teeth between hubs and sleeve.

## How it works
External gear teeth on hubs mesh with internal teeth in a sleeve; crowned teeth allow angular/parallel misalignment. Lubricated; high torque density.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Torque capacity very high.
- Misalignment limited (~4-5 deg angular).
- Torsionally stiff; backlash small.

## Constraints
- Requires lubrication.
- Wear over time.
- Alignment more critical than elastomeric.

## Failure modes
- Tooth wear/pitting.
- Lubrication failure.
- Fretting/corrosion.

## When to use
Turbines, compressors, rolling mills, marine, heavy drives.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use commercial units; lubricate; inspect teeth periodically.
