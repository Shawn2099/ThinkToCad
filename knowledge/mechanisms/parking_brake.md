# parking_brake

**Type:** brake
**Function:** Mechanically (cable) or electronically hold a vehicle stationary, often via drum-in-hat or shoe.

## How it works
A mechanical cable or electronic actuator applies a holding brake (drum-in-hat or shoe) independent of service brakes. Rarely effective for high-speed stops.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Holding torque must exceed max grade torque.
- Cable tension/actuator force sets clamp.
- Drum-in-hat provides independent holding surface.

## Constraints
- Holding only; not for high-speed stops.
- Cable stretch/adjustment needed.
- Electronic systems need power.

## Failure modes
- Cable corrosion/stretch.
- Inadequate holding on steep grades.
- Seizure from corrosion.

## When to use
Vehicle parking/emergency holding.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use corrosion-resistant cables; provide adjustment; test holding on max grade.
