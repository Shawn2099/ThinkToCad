# chain_coupling

**Type:** coupling
**Function:** Connect two sprocket hubs with a roller chain, accommodating some misalignment.

## How it works
Two sprocket hubs are linked by a roller chain (single/double strand); torque transmits via chain; double strand accommodates more misalignment.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Torque moderate to high.
- Speed limited by chain rating.
- Misalignment capacity depends on strand count.

## Constraints
- Requires lubrication.
- Wear and noise.
- No overload protection unless designed.

## Failure modes
- Chain wear/elongation.
- Sprocket tooth wear.
- Lubrication failure.

## When to use
Conveyors, agricultural, general industrial drives.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use standard chain/sprockets; provide lubrication; allow tension adjustment.
