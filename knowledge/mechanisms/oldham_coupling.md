# oldham_coupling

**Type:** coupling
**Function:** Connect shafts with parallel offset misalignment using three discs with perpendicular tongue-and-groove.

## How it works
Two slotted (tongued) plates on the shafts plus a central disc with perpendicular tongues; the central disc slides in both slots, accommodating parallel offset. Invented 1821 by John Oldham.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Handles parallel misalignment; limited angular misalignment.
- Torque transmitted through sliding contact; friction/wear at disc.
- Compact for given offset.

## Constraints
- Sliding contact -> wear and need for lubrication.
- Limited speed and offset.
- Backlash from slot clearances.

## Failure modes
- Wear of tongues/slots.
- Lubrication failure causing seizure.
- Vibration at high speed/offset.

## When to use
Parallel-offset shafts, pumps, compact drives.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use hardened tongues/slots, lubricate, limit offset and speed.
