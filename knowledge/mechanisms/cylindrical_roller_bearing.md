# cylindrical_roller_bearing

**Type:** bearing
**Function:** Cylindrical rollers between races; high radial capacity, low axial - [Wikipedia, Rolling-element bearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling-element_bearing).

## How it works
Cylindrical rollers between races; high radial capacity, low axial - [Wikipedia, Rolling-element bearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling-element_bearing).

## Key dimensions / equations
- High radial; low axial; sensitive to misalignment.

## Constraints
- Poor misalignment tolerance
- low axial.

## Failure modes
- Poor misalignment tolerance, low axial.

## When to use
- Applications: Gearboxes, electric motors, heavy machinery.
- Select when: High radial load, good alignment.
- Advantages: High radial load, high speed (some).

## DFM (small workshop)
Use standard parts and simple tooling; verify fit and tolerances in the small workshop.
