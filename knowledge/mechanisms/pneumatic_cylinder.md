# pneumatic_cylinder

**Type:** actuator
**Function:** Compressed air -> linear/rotary.

## How it works
Compressed air -> linear/rotary. Pressure 80-120 PSI, bore, stroke

## Key dimensions / equations
- Pressure 80-120 PSI, bore, stroke

## Constraints
- Low precision (compressible air)
- lower force

## Failure modes
- Low precision (compressible air); lower force

## When to use
- Applications: Automation, packaging, clamping, valves - [LINAK](https://www.linak-us.com/products/linear-actuators/what-is-an-actuator)
- Advantages: Fast; simple; low cost; clean

## DFM (small workshop)
Use standard parts and simple tooling; verify fit and tolerances in the small workshop.
