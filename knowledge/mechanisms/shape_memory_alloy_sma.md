# shape_memory_alloy_sma

**Type:** actuator
**Function:** Heat (Joule) -> shape recovery.

## How it works
Heat (Joule) -> shape recovery. Transformation temps As/Af/Ms/Mf; strain 4-8%; stress 200 MPa

## Key dimensions / equations
- Transformation temps As/Af/Ms/Mf; strain 4-8%; stress 200 MPa

## Constraints
- Slow cooling
- hysteresis
- fatigue
- low efficiency

## Failure modes
- Slow cooling; hysteresis; fatigue; low efficiency

## When to use
- Applications: Soft robots, wearable devices, biomedical, aerospace - [Wiley](https://advanced.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/adma.202208517); [MDPI](https://www.mdpi.com/2076-0825/14/5/248)
- Advantages: High energy density; large force/weight; compact; silent

## DFM (small workshop)
Use standard parts and simple tooling; verify fit and tolerances in the small workshop.
