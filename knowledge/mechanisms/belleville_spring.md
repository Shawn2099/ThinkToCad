# belleville_spring

**Type:** spring
**Function:** Provide high force in a compact axial space using a conical (disc) spring washer.

## How it works
A conical disc (Belleville washer) deforms axially under load, storing energy. Stacking (series/parallel) tailors load-deflection. Very stiff; large force in small space.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Load-deflection characteristic depends on cone angle, thickness, diameter.
- Stacking in series increases deflection; in parallel increases load.
- High spring rate; nonlinear for large deflections.

## Constraints
- Sensitive to flatness and friction between stacked discs.
- Buckling/hysteresis if not guided.
- Limited deflection (typically 0.2-0.8x thickness).

## Failure modes
- Fatigue from cyclic loading.
- Permanent set if overstressed.
- Stiction in stacked assemblies.

## When to use
Bolted joints (preload), clutches, valves, high-force compact applications.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use standard Belleville washers, stack for required load/deflection, ensure flat seating and lubrication.
