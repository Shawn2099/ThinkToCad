# detent

**Type:** indexing
**Function:** Resist or arrest movement at discrete positions using a spring-loaded ball/pin or magnetic means.

## How it works
A spring-loaded ball detent engages incremental depressions (or spring steel snaps into notches); magnetic detents use stepper-motor-like attraction. Holding force F = k*x (spring preload); release when applied torque exceeds mu*F*r (ball radius).

## Key dimensions / equations
- Holding force F = k*x (spring preload at engagement).
- Release torque threshold ~ mu*F*r (ball radius r).
- Use hardened balls/pins and case-hardened seats for wear.

## Constraints
- Wear at ball/seat interface.
- Holding force limited by spring size.
- Magnetic detents avoid contact wear/contamination.

## Failure modes
- Seat wear reducing holding force.
- Spring fatigue.
- Contamination causing sticking.

## When to use
Positioning, indexing, knobs, rotary switches, light detents.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use standard detent springs/balls, case-harden seats, lubricate; magnetic for clean/low-wear.
