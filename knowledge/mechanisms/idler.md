# idler

**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** Free-spinning pulley/sprocket that changes belt/chain path, increases wrap angle, and reduces vibration.

## How it works
An idler redirects belt/chain, increasing contact/wrap angle and damping vibrations. Can be smooth or toothed.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Wrap angle increase raises power capacity.
- Bearing load depends on tension.
- Diameter affects belt flexing.

## Constraints
- Bearing wear.
- Alignment required.
- Added friction.

## Failure modes
- Bearing failure.
- Misalignment wear.
- Belt tracking issues.

## When to use
Automotive accessory drives, long belt runs.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use sealed bearings; align with belt; provide adjustment.
