# blind_rivet_pop_rivet

**Type:** fastener
**Function:** Tubular rivet with mandrel; installed from one side; mandrel breaks after expansion - [Wikipedia, Rivet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rivet).

## How it works
Tubular rivet with mandrel; installed from one side; mandrel breaks after expansion - [Wikipedia, Rivet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rivet).

## Key dimensions / equations
- Light-to-moderate shear/tension; structural versions higher.

## Constraints
- Lower strength than solid
- mandrel may loosen
- hole size critical.

## Failure modes
- Lower strength than solid; mandrel may loosen; hole size critical.

## When to use
- Applications: Sheet metal, enclosures, automotive panels.
- Select when: One-side access, moderate loads, speed.
- Advantages: One-side access, fast, simple tools.
