# hydrodynamic_journal_bearing

**Type:** bearing
**Function:** Fluid film separates surfaces via shaft rotation; full-film lubrication - [Wikipedia, Plain bearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plain_bearing) (fluid lubrication).

## How it works
Fluid film separates surfaces via shaft rotation; full-film lubrication - [Wikipedia, Plain bearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plain_bearing) (fluid lubrication).

## Key dimensions / equations
- Very high; limited by oil whirl/whip.

## Constraints
- Needs rotation to lift
- oil whirl risk
- start/stop wear.

## Failure modes
- Needs rotation to lift; oil whirl risk; start/stop wear.

## When to use
- Applications: Turbomachinery, large engines, turbines.
- Select when: High speed, high load, continuous operation.
- Advantages: Very high load, low friction at speed, long life.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use standard parts and simple tooling; verify fit and tolerances in the small workshop.
