## How it works
Smooth rounded head with square neck under head that bites into material to prevent rotation; used with nut - [Wikipedia, Bolt (fastener)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolt_(fastener)); [Fastener Technical Manual (DIN 603)](https://classccomponents.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Fastener-Technical-Manual.pdf).

## Key dimensions / equations
- Moderate tension; shear via shank; good in wood/soft materials.

## Constraints
- Requires square hole/soft material
- lower strength than hex bolts.

## Failure modes
- Requires square hole/soft material; lower strength than hex bolts.

## When to use
- Applications: Wood structures, wagons, fencing, docks.
- Select when: Wood/soft materials, smooth exposed head, one-side tooling.
- Advantages: Tamper-resistant (no external drive), self-locking against rotation, smooth head.
