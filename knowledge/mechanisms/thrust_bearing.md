# thrust_bearing

**Type:** bearing
**Function:** Support axial (thrust) loads along a shaft.

## How it works
Bearing surfaces oriented perpendicular to shaft axis to carry axial load. Variants: thrust ball, thrust roller, flat thrust (washer) bearing, and tapered-roller (combined). Often used with radial bearings.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Basic rating life L10 = (C/P)^p (p = 3 ball, 10/3 roller) for rolling thrust bearings.
- Thrust washers: PV and material limits; lubrication critical.
- Fits per ISO 286.

## Constraints
- Pure thrust bearings carry little/no radial load (except tapered rollers).
- Speed limits; high speed needs oil lubrication.
- Requires axial location/retention.

## Failure modes
- Fatigue pitting under cyclic thrust.
- Overheating at high speed.
- Wear/scoring in thrust washers.

## When to use
Gearbox axial loads, propeller shafts, vertical shafts, screw jacks.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use thrust washers for low speed; ball/roller thrust bearings for higher loads; ensure lubrication and retention.
