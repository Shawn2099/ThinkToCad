# magnetostrictive

**Type:** actuator
**Function:** Magnetic field -> strain.

## How it works
Magnetic field -> strain. Field strength, alloy (Terfenol-D)

## Key dimensions / equations
- Field strength, alloy (Terfenol-D)

## Constraints
- High cost
- eddy currents

## Failure modes
- High cost; eddy currents

## When to use
- Applications: Sonar, precision positioning - [comparative](https://bdml.stanford.edu/twiki/pub/Main/PerchingLinks/Hollerbach_Hunter_Ballantyne_1992_A_Comparative_Analysis_of_Actuator_Technologies_for_Robotics.pdf)
- Advantages: High bandwidth; force

## DFM (small workshop)
Use standard parts and simple tooling; verify fit and tolerances in the small workshop.
