# tensioner

**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** Maintain belt/chain tension automatically or manually, absorbing load and temperature changes.

## How it works
Uses eccentric, spring-loaded, adjustable-arm, or automatic (hydraulic/spring) elements to keep tension within range and extend belt life.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Tension set per belt manufacturer (typically 1-2% of breaking strength).
- Spring force vs. deflection.
- Travel must accommodate stretch/temperature.

## Constraints
- Added complexity.
- Spring fatigue.
- Maintenance.

## Failure modes
- Spring failure.
- Bearing wear.
- Loss of tension setting.

## When to use
Automotive, conveyors, industrial belt drives with long belts/variable loads.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use commercial tensioners; provide access for adjustment; protect springs.
