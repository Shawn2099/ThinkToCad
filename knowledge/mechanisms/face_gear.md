# face_gear
**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** Cylindrical pinion meshes with teeth on face of disk for right-angle drives.

## How it works
Pinion (spur/helical) meshes with face gear teeth on disk surface.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Face width; pressure angle; offset.

## Constraints
- Manufacturing complexity.
- Alignment.

## Failure modes
- Tooth wear.
- Edge loading.

## When to use
Right-angle drives, aerospace.
