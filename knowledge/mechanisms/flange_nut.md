# flange_nut

**Type:** fastener
**Function:** Hex nut with integral washer flange (sometimes serrated) to distribute load and resist loosening - [Fastener Technical Manual (DIN 6923/ISO 4161)](https://classccomponents.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Fastener-Technical-Manual.pdf).

## How it works
Hex nut with integral washer flange (sometimes serrated) to distribute load and resist loosening - [Fastener Technical Manual (DIN 6923/ISO 4161)](https://classccomponents.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Fastener-Technical-Manual.pdf).

## Key dimensions / equations
- Moderate; larger bearing area than hex nut.

## Constraints
- Flange can embed in soft materials
- not for extreme loads.

## Failure modes
- Flange can embed in soft materials; not for extreme loads.

## When to use
- Applications: Automotive, sheet metal, general assembly.
- Select when: Need load distribution without separate washer.
- Advantages: No separate washer, faster assembly, some locking via serrations.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use standard parts and simple tooling; verify fit and tolerances in the small workshop.
