# follower_roller

**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** Reduce friction and wear between cam and follower by using a rolling element.

## How it works
A roller (wheel) mounted on a pin/bearing rides on the cam profile instead of sliding. Converts sliding contact to rolling, lowering friction and wear; requires precise alignment and adds cost/size.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Prime circle radius = base circle radius + roller radius.
- Pitch curve = radial displacements applied to prime circle.
- Pressure angle limits (<=30deg/35deg) still apply; roller radius affects curvature/undercutting.

## Constraints
- Roller must be larger than profile curvature variations to avoid undercutting.
- Needs bearing/seal; higher cost and size.
- Misalignment causes edge loading and rapid wear.

## Failure modes
- Bearing failure in roller.
- Edge loading from misalignment.
- Seizure if lubrication fails.

## When to use
High-speed cams, heavy loads, long-life applications, precision machinery.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use off-the-shelf needle bearings or hardened rollers; ensure alignment; lubricate.
