## Key dimensions / equations
- High tension; avoids repeated thread wear in parent part.

## Constraints
- Requires tapped hole
- removal can be difficult.

## Failure modes
- Requires tapped hole; removal can be difficult.

## When to use
- Applications: Engine blocks, flanges, pressure vessels.
- Select when: Frequent disassembly, threaded holes in soft/cast parts.
- Advantages: Repeated assembly without damaging base threads; precise preload.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use standard parts and simple tooling; verify fit and tolerances in the small workshop.
