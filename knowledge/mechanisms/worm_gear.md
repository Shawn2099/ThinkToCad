# worm_gear

**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** Provide high single-stage reduction (10:1-500:1) between skew shafts, often self-locking.

## How it works
A screw-like worm meshes with a worm wheel (gear). The worm's lead angle determines ratio and self-locking: small lead angle -> self-locking (worm cannot be driven by wheel). Double-enveloping (cone-drive) gives more contact.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Ratio = teeth on wheel / starts on worm.
- Self-locking when lead angle small (friction prevents back-driving).
- Efficiency varies with lead angle (typically 40-90%).
- Right/left-handed worms available.

## Constraints
- Sliding friction -> low efficiency and heat; needs good lubrication.
- Self-locking prevents back-driving (safety) but also prevents regeneration.
- Bronze wheel + steel worm typical to avoid galling.

## Failure modes
- Overheating from friction.
- Worm wheel wear (bronze).
- Lubrication breakdown causing seizure.

## When to use
High reduction in compact space, hoists, conveyors, indexing, safety (self-locking) applications.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use bronze wheel + hardened steel worm, oil bath lubrication, avoid overloading, allow cooling.
