# anti_parallelogram_linkage
**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** Couple two shafts to rotate in opposite directions via crossed equal links.

## How it works
Four-bar with opposite links equal but crossed; coupler rotates in the opposite sense to input.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Equal link lengths; crossed configuration.
- Crossing clearance between links.

## Constraints
- Link interference at crossing.
- Limited travel range.

## Failure modes
- Crossing links collide.
- Joint wear.

## When to use
Coupled opposite rotation; folding/ornamental mechanisms.

## DFM (small workshop)
Add spacers at crossing; use bushings; check clearance.
