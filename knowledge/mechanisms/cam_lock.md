# cam_lock

**Type:** indexing
**Function:** Latch a door/cabinet/tool chest using a base plus a cam rotated by a key or tool.

## How it works
A cam (straight or offset/reversible) rotates with the key; in locked position the cam blocks the keeper/strike, preventing opening. Simple, low-cost locking.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Cam profile (straight or offset) determines lock/unlock rotation angle.
- Torque to operate depends on friction and cam geometry.
- Often 90deg or 180deg rotation between lock/unlock.

## Constraints
- Security limited (simple cam).
- Cam wear reduces latching.
- Keeper alignment critical.

## Failure modes
- Cam wear or deformation.
- Keeper misalignment preventing lock.
- Key/cam shear under forced entry.

## When to use
Cabinets, tool chests, enclosures, light security.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use standard cam locks, align keeper, lubricate, consider offset (reversible) cams.
