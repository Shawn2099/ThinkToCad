# adhesive_bonded_joint

**Type:** joint
**Function:** Load transferred through thin adhesive layer over overlap; strong in shear, weak in peel - [Ideambox](https://ideambox.com/assets/downloads/welded-adhesive-joints.pdf); [KEVOS](https://kevos.com/insights/choosing-a-joining-method/).

## How it works
Load transferred through thin adhesive layer over overlap; strong in shear, weak in peel - [Ideambox](https://ideambox.com/assets/downloads/welded-adhesive-joints.pdf); [KEVOS](https://kevos.com/insights/choosing-a-joining-method/).

## Key dimensions / equations
- Shear across bonded area; e.g. 25x12.5 mm overlap at 15 MPa ~= 4.7 kN - [KEVOS](https://kevos.com/insights/choosing-a-joining-method/).

## Constraints
- Surface prep critical
- cure time
- temperature limits
- weak peel.

## Failure modes
- Surface prep critical, cure time, temperature limits, weak peel.

## When to use
- Applications: Automotive bodies, aircraft panels, plastics/composites.
- Select when: Thin/dissimilar materials, sealed/smooth joints.
- Advantages: No holes, spreads load, seals, mixed materials.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use standard parts and simple tooling; verify fit and tolerances in the small workshop.
