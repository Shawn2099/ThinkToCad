# gear
**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** Transmit or convert rotary motion between shafts with ratio via toothed wheels.

## How it works
Meshing gears transfer torque; ratio equals teeth count ratio.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Module, teeth count, ratio, pressure angle.

## Constraints
- Backlash; noise; lubrication; mounting precision.

## Failure modes
- Tooth wear/pitting.
- Backlash growth.

## When to use
Speed/torque conversion, direction change.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use standard modules; ensure alignment.
