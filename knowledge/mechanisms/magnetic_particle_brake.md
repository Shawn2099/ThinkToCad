# magnetic_particle_brake

**Type:** brake
**Function:** Provide adjustable braking torque via magnetic particles aligning under a magnetic field.

## How it works
Magnetic particles in a cavity align under DC excitation, creating shear resistance between rotor and housing. Torque proportional to field strength; allows slip.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Torque proportional to coil current.
- Slip power = T * omega; thermal limit.
- Response depends on inductance.

## Constraints
- Electrical power required.
- Heat under continuous slip.
- Lower torque density than friction.

## Failure modes
- Particle degradation.
- Coil overheating.
- Seal leakage.

## When to use
Tension control, dynamometers, adjustable retarders.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use commercial units; ventilate; limit slip speed.
