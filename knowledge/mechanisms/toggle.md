# toggle

**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** Provide enormous (theoretically infinite) mechanical advantage near the end of stroke and self-lock in the straight-line position.

## How it works
Two links meeting at a central pivot near 180deg; as links approach collinearity, mechanical advantage grows toward infinity, locking the mechanism. Used as clamps, presses, crushers.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Toggle positions occur when two moving links are collinear (four-bar).
- Mechanical advantage MA = F_out/F_in -> inf as transmission angle -> 0deg/180deg.
- Time/force relation: small input motion near toggle produces large holding force.

## Constraints
- Only high advantage near end of stroke; low advantage elsewhere.
- Must be driven past center to lock; release requires pushing past center again.
- Links and pivot must be stiff to avoid snap-through.

## Failure modes
- Accidental release under vibration if not clearly over center.
- Link/pivot bending or buckling under high toggle force.
- Pin wear at central pivot.

## When to use
Toggle clamps, rock crushers, riveting machines, injection molding, press tools.

## DFM (small workshop)
Ensure toggle passes clearly over center, use robust pins, add stops, guard pinch points.
