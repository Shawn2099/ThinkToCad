# follower_flat_faced

**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** Provide a simple, low-cost cam follower with a flat contact face (mushroom follower).

## How it works
A flat-faced (mushroom) follower slides on the cam profile; contact is a line/point on the flat face. Cheaper and more compact than roller, but higher friction and wear; suitable for low speed/load.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Pressure angle limits (<=30deg translating / <=35deg rotating).
- Contact stress depends on cam curvature and follower material.
- Fundamental law (continuous s, v, a; finite jerk) applies.

## Constraints
- Higher friction/wear than roller; needs lubrication.
- Flat face can bind if pressure angle too high.
- Face must be kept flat and true.

## Failure modes
- Scuffing/galling of face and cam.
- Binding at high pressure angle.
- Face wear changing profile.

## When to use
Low-speed, low-load, cost-sensitive cams; simple mechanisms.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use hardened cam and follower, lubricate, keep speeds low, prefer flat-machined faces.
