# shaft

**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** Rotating member that transmits power and motion and supports gears, pulleys, sprockets, and couplings.

## How it works
A (usually circular) shaft carries torque and bending loads; transmits power between components. Sized for torque, bending, and critical speed.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Shear stress tau = 16 T / (pi d^3) (torsion).
- Bending stress sigma = 32 M / (pi d^3).
- Critical speed depends on stiffness and mass distribution.

## Constraints
- Fatigue from cyclic loading.
- Misalignment induces bending.
- Corrosion/environment.

## Failure modes
- Fatigue fracture.
- Excessive deflection/vibration.
- Wear at bearing/seal surfaces.

## When to use
All rotary power trains; size per torque/bending/critical speed.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use standard round bar; add keyways with proper fillets; avoid sharp transitions.
