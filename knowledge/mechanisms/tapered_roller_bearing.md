# tapered_roller_bearing

**Type:** bearing
**Function:** Conical rollers/races support both radial and axial; common in vehicles - [Wikipedia, Rolling-element bearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling-element_bearing).

## How it works
Conical rollers/races support both radial and axial; common in vehicles - [Wikipedia, Rolling-element bearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling-element_bearing).

## Key dimensions / equations
- High combined radial+axial.

## Constraints
- More expensive
- friction from collar
- needs preload.

## Failure modes
- More expensive, friction from collar, needs preload.

## When to use
- Applications: Wheel hubs, gearboxes, differentials.
- Select when: Combined loads, automotive, heavy.
- Advantages: High load, handles cornering loads.

Use standard parts and simple tooling; verify fit and tolerances in the small workshop.
