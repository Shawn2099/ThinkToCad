# chebyshev_linkage
**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** Approximate straight-line motion from rotary input via four-bar proportions.

## How it works
Four-bar with Chebyshev proportions; coupler point traces a near-straight segment.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Link ratios (approx 1:2:2.5).
- Stroke length.

## Constraints
- Approximate straight line.
- Limited stroke.

## Failure modes
- Deviation from straight line.
- Joint wear.

## When to use
Leg mechanisms, walking machines.

## DFM (small workshop)
Prototype and measure deviation; adjust pivots.
