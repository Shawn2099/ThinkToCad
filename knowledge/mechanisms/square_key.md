## How it works
Square cross-section key seated in shaft/keyway transmitting torque - [Engineering Hardware catalog](https://engineeringhardware.com/); [Cromwell](https://www.cromwell.co.uk/shop/fasteners-and-fixings/rivets-pins-and-keys/c/2342).

## Key dimensions / equations
- Torque transmission; key shears as weak link (protects drivetrain).

## Constraints
- Stress concentration
- loosening possible
- not for high speed.

## Failure modes
- Stress concentration; loosening possible; not for high speed.

## When to use
- Applications: Gears, pulleys, couplings on shafts.
- Select when: Torque transmission, sacrificial weak link desired.
- Advantages: Standard, high torque, simple.

Use standard parts and simple tooling; verify fit and tolerances in the small workshop.
