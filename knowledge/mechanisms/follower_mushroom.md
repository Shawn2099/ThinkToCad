# follower_mushroom
**Type:** mechanism
**Function:** Follow cam with large mushroom head for high load distribution.

## How it works
Mushroom follower has wide head; distributes load.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Head diameter; load rating.

## Constraints
- Size; mass.

## Failure modes
- Wear; fatigue.

## When to use
High-load cams.

## DFM (small workshop)
Harden head; lubricate.

## Sources
- Standard classification (Norton/Shigley).
