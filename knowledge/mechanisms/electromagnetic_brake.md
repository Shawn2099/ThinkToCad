# electromagnetic_brake

**Type:** brake
**Function:** Stop or hold motion using an electrically actuated friction or particle/hysteresis element.

## How it works
An electromagnet clamps friction surfaces or aligns particles to create braking torque when energized (or de-energized for spring-applied). Fast response; ~80% of power-applied brakes.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Torque proportional to coil current and friction interfaces.
- Spring-applied (fail-safe) vs. power-applied types.
- Thermal limit from slip energy.

## Constraints
- Requires electrical power/control.
- Heat buildup under slipping.
- Torque vs. current vendor-specific.

## Failure modes
- Coil burnout.
- Friction surface wear.
- Air gap change.

## When to use
Motor-integrated braking, retarders, safety/holding brakes.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use commercial units; provide ventilation/fusing; verify fail-safe mode.
