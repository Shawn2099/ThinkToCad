# rubber_spring

**Type:** spring
**Function:** Absorb shock and isolate vibration using elastomeric blocks loaded in shear and/or compression.

## How it works
Rubber/urethane elements deform under load, storing energy and providing damping through internal hysteresis. Geometry (shear/compression) sets stiffness and damping.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Stiffness depends on shear modulus G, bonded area, and shape factor.
- Damping from material hysteresis (loss factor).
- Nonlinear force-deflection; frequency-dependent.

## Constraints
- Temperature and chemical environment affect properties.
- Creep/set under sustained load.
- Bonding to metal inserts required.

## Failure modes
- Tear or debonding from metal.
- Hardening/aging (ozone, UV, heat).
- Overload causing permanent set.

## When to use
Vibration isolation, bump stops, shock absorption, engine mounts.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use commercial elastomeric mounts; bond with proper adhesive; keep within temperature/chemical limits.
