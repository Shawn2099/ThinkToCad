# hydrostatic_bearing

**Type:** bearing
**Function:** Externally pumped fluid film; supports load at zero speed - [Wikipedia, Plain bearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plain_bearing) (fluid lubrication).

## How it works
Externally pumped fluid film; supports load at zero speed - [Wikipedia, Plain bearing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plain_bearing) (fluid lubrication).

## Key dimensions / equations
- Very high; stiffness high.

## Constraints
- Pump/complexity
- contamination sensitivity
- cost.

## Failure modes
- Pump/complexity, contamination sensitivity, cost.

## When to use
- Applications: Precision machines, heavy spindles, CMMs.
- Select when: Precision, heavy load, low speed/start-stop.
- Advantages: Zero friction at zero speed, very stiff, high load.

Use standard parts and simple tooling; verify fit and tolerances in the small workshop.
