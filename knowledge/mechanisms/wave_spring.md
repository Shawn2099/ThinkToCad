# wave_spring

**Type:** spring
**Function:** Deliver high force in very small axial space using a flat wire marcelled into a wavy pattern.

## How it works
Flat wire is formed into waves (single/multi-turn, linear, or interlaced) so axial compression flattens waves, producing force. Stacking waves multiplies load in minimal height.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Force proportional to wave amplitude, wire thickness, and number of waves/turns.
- Compact axial height vs. coil spring.
- Deflection modest for high load.

## Constraints
- Limited deflection per turn; stack for more travel.
- Sensitive to buckling if unsupported.
- Manufacturing requires precise forming.

## Failure modes
- Wire fatigue at wave crests.
- Buckling or sideways extrusion.
- Over-compression causing permanent set.

## When to use
Bearing preload, valve wear compensation, space-constrained axial loading.

## DFM (small workshop)
Use commercial wave washers/springs; ensure guided compression and correct orientation.
