## Key dimensions / equations

## Constraints
- Socket can strip
- needs hex key
- less torque than hex head for large sizes.

## Failure modes
- Socket can strip; needs hex key; less torque than hex head for large sizes.

## When to use
- Applications: Precision machinery, tooling, fixtures, aerospace (varies).
- Select when: Limited wrench clearance, high strength, compact assembly.
- Advantages: Compact, high strength, clean appearance, recessed drive.
