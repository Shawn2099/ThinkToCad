# volute_spring

**Type:** spring
**Function:** Provide long travel and progressive (variable) rate in a compact axial space using a flat strip wound conically.

## How it works
A wide thin strip is wound flat so coils nest inside one another (conical form). Under load coils nest/stack, giving longer travel and rising stiffness than a simple conical helical spring.

## Key dimensions / equations
- Rate rises as coils nest; geometry-driven (strip width/thickness, cone angle).
- Travel exceeds equivalent helical conical spring.
- Load capacity depends on strip cross-section.

## Constraints
- More complex to design/manufacture than helical springs.
- Nesting requires precise coil geometry.
- Limited to compression.

## Failure modes
- Coil binding or mis-nesting.
- Strip buckling under high load.
- Fatigue at strip edges.

## When to use
Variable-rate scales, vehicle suspension (e.g., Stuart tank), compact long-travel compression.

## DFM (small workshop)
Prefer commercial units; if fabricating, form strip on a mandrel and test travel carefully.
